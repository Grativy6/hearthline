# Artifact History and Artifacts

## `HEARTHLINE/IMAGE-000002` — Prismatic twin-spiral Rainbow Shell

![Prismatic twin-spiral Rainbow Shell source](prismatic-twin-spiral-rainbow-shell-source.png)

Two mirrored spirals share one golden circumference and leave a dark, breathing opening between them. This was the first Rainbow Shell image: not yet a staff crown, not yet joined to Mira's loupe, and not yet called **Holds Nothing Back**. Its empty center mattered from the beginning. Hearthline has always distrusted artifacts that pretend fullness means having nowhere left for the unknown to enter.

Status: `SOURCE_ARTIFACT`. Its later synthesis appears in the [current artifact room](../).

The image is a fictional design source and visual provenance record, not a deployed device, executable specification, or grant of capability.
