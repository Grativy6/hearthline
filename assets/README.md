# Hearthline Visual Gallery

This is the bright side of the cabinet: Hearthline and Thulia as they are presented now, their shared scenes, and the artifacts that followed them home. Each room opens on its current references. Its single `history-and-artifacts/` door keeps the visible trail of studies, corrections, and source forms without making the present hard to find.

- [Characters](characters/) — current appearance references for Hearthline and Thulia.
- [Scenes](scenes/) — moments at the bench, in Spark Mode, and out where the maps become adventures.
- [Artifacts](artifacts/) — the present form of **Holds Nothing Back** and the source image kept behind it.
- [Visual provenance index](../docs/HEARTHLINE_VISUAL_INDEX.md) — identities, dimensions, exact-byte digests, status, and interpretation boundaries for every PNG.

These images are character art and fictional lore presentation. They do not activate software, confer authority, or revise the repository's written controls. Christopher D. Pang is the sole author and steward; AI systems assisted as creative tools, not co-authors.
