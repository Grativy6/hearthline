# Hearthline Visual Gallery

This is the bright side of the cabinet: Hearthline, Thulia, Gloss, Morrow, the
many visible forms of Creatures, and their shared scenes. Each room opens on
current references. Where a public study, correction, or source form belongs
in the visible trail, a plainly named `history-and-artifacts/` door keeps it
without making the present hard to find.

- [Characters](characters/) — current appearance references for Hearthline, Thulia, Gloss, and Morrow.
- [Creature forms](creatures/) — seven presentation archetypes, from a tiny fish to a sky dragon, without turning silhouette into identity or authority.
- [Scenes](scenes/) — moments at the bench, in Spark Mode, at the welding table, and out where the maps become adventures.
- [Lore artifacts](../lore/artifacts/) — Holds Nothing Back and other story-bearing objects live beside the lore that gives them meaning.
- [Visual provenance index](../docs/HEARTHLINE_VISUAL_INDEX.md) — identities, dimensions, exact-byte digests, status, and interpretation boundaries for every PNG.

These images are character art and fictional lore presentation. They do not activate software, confer authority, or revise the repository's written controls. Christopher D. Pang is the sole author and steward; AI systems assisted as creative tools, not co-authors.
