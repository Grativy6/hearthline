# Hearthline Visual Assets

This directory preserves Hearthline and Thulia visual material under stable, append-only image identities. The identities in this first archival set were assigned retrospectively to already-created images before repository publication; they are not claimed to have been preallocated before generation. Corrections receive new numbers, and earlier studies remain available with their residuals recorded in the [Hearthline Visual Index](../docs/HEARTHLINE_VISUAL_INDEX.md).

## Directory map

- `characters/hearthline/` — Hearthline appearance and presentation references
- `characters/thulia/` — Thulia portraits, studies, and construction sheets
- `scenes/` — illustrations involving more than one character or a narrative setting

## Naming and preservation

The filename mirrors the canonical readable identity by replacing `/` with `-`, followed by a short descriptive slug.

A future image receives the next ordinal in its own series before repository publication. Do not reuse an ordinal, overwrite an earlier file, or rename old images to make a later correction appear original. Rejected, superseded, or imperfect studies remain preserved with their status and residuals.

Image order is separate from character-sheet and profile order. An image cannot revise a `SHEET`, `PROFILE`, operating document, or role boundary.

## Source and generation status

`HEARTHLINE/IMAGE-000001` was supplied as an existing reference image. Its exact generator and production history are not established by the file.

`OWL-000001/IMAGE-000001` through `IMAGE-000007` are OpenAI image-generation outputs created during the September 2, 2026 design session under Christopher D. Pang's direction and selection. Exact backend model identifiers and complete prompts are not embedded in the PNGs. AI systems assisted with production; they are not co-authors or authorities.

## Control and residuals

Images are visual references, not independent specifications. Thulia's written role document controls behavior, and her latest adopted written character sheet controls appearance. `OWL-000001/IMAGE-000006` is the corrected bilateral visual aid; earlier images remain studies or superseded correction evidence. See the visual index for exact laterality and Northlight residuals.
