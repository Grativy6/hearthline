# Hearthline

### Trace-informed systems lab

Hearthline is a model-neutral toolkit for carrying sources, distinctions,
uncertainty and recoverable work across tasks. This repository holds its
public MCP, technical contracts, tests and scoped formal companions.

**Love; Seed, not Feed; Honest.** The [founding orientation](docs/FOUNDING_ORIENTATION.md)
offers room to build while preserving source and authority boundaries.

## Formal work and its limits

[**Context Parallax v1.0**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23306102) is the published research preprint, with its downloadable paper and reproducibility package.

The CPX companion contains 33 checked declarations in five Lean modules,
covering four selected claim families. Its results identify exact dependencies,
axiom use, failed attempts, kernel replay and the limits of the source-to-formal
self-review. Those results do not verify the entire manuscript or its empirical
hypotheses. Other audits retain their own source versions and evidence scopes.

## The toolkit

The MCP supplies bounded profiles for context, continuity, correction,
observation, science and software work. Its foundation includes PAL v2.4
checks while preserving PAL v2.3 and earlier profiles and the separately sourced CHARTER,
PECAN, PEA and SEED boundaries. Implementation profiles are not a claim of
whole-system conformance.

The public package works independently of personal lore, private memories,
character art or a particular model. Optional personal context belongs in a
separately configured environment. No account, authority or private memory is
created by reading these documents or loading a template.

The toolkit does not install Chris's personal version of Hearthline. Choose
your own model, voice and context. The [public history](CHANGELOG.md) remains
available if you want to explore where the work came from.

The [design contract index](docs/DESIGN_CONTRACTS.md) explains the named mechanisms. In the [Return Queue](docs/HEARTHLINE_RETURN_QUEUE.md), only the controller owns counts, commits an order, and admits service.

Tests live in `tests/`; structural checks live in `tools/`. [Verification instructions](docs/VERIFYING.md) cover toolkit tests, frozen evidence and public history. Each research
companion states what was checked and how to reopen it. Technical design
proposals remain labeled as proposals wherever an implementation has not been
established.

## Stewardship and source

Christopher D. Pang is Hearthline's author and original steward. Source maps,
artifact-specific credits and exact audit scopes carry the provenance of the
published work. A successful check is evidence for its stated conditions; it
does not grant permission or confer authority.

Existing [license](LICENSE) and [name/mark](TRADEMARKS.md) terms remain in force
for the material to which they apply. Referenced works keep their own notices.
