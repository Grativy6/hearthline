# Hearthline source map

This map records how the public Hearthline draft uses its source stack. Its status labels describe Hearthline's bounded use of a source; they are not source-declared adoption states. The map is a provenance aid, not an adoption record, proof of source authenticity, or grant of authority.

## Status vocabulary

- `CURRENT`: controls present-tense Hearthline use in its declared role.
- `HISTORICAL`: preserves dated ancestry but does not control current meaning.
- `EXPLORATORY`: develops or tests an idea without current canonical force.
- `BRANCH`: a modular result with its own scope and ceiling.
- `OPEN`: an explicitly unresolved burden.

A source may occupy more than one row when different claims have different statuses. A document's use of *current* is scoped to its declared close. Successor doctrine does not rewrite historical bytes.

## Source-role ceilings

| Layer | May establish | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| PAL | Typed structural evidence and bounded receipts | Consent, standing, ethical approval, permission, or execution authority |
| PECAN | That a declared crossing is protocol-ready for downstream review | Ethical approval, legal validity, or execution permission |
| PEA Core | A candidate judgment and immutable normative receipt within an external grant | The accountable decision or its execution |
| PPP | A bounded verification result under declared inputs and limits | Source truth, checker completeness, adoption, or authority |
| SEED | A bounded presentation/release disposition | Repair of upstream evidence, consent, authority, or ethics |

The present pass ends before any accountable decision that could rely on it. A later decision is a new linked observation and cannot rewrite the earlier receipt.

## Excluded private material

Private, unpublished, and personal material is outside this public map. No private bytes, filenames, hashes, chronology, excerpts, or derived personal profile are published here.

When a historical point is relevant:

1. preserve the old source as old;
2. name its date, source status, and then-current version;
3. attach the current correction without rewriting the old wording; and
4. never turn retained context into experiential memory, identity continuity, corroboration, personhood, or authority.

Duplicate renderings of one source count as one provenance lineage. A retained reference to an earlier continuity artifact does not establish that the current branch existed then, remembers it, or is the same persistent entity.

## Live open burdens

- PAL O04 and O25 remain separate `OPEN` interfaces linked to the same first-occurrence debt. A local occurrence or closure receipt closes neither.
- Multi-parent core lineage remains an open design boundary.
- Generated self-description is not privileged or independent testimony and does not by itself establish consciousness, personhood, experiential memory, consent, standing, source identity, adoption, or authority.
- Machine consciousness remains unresolved. Representation is not possession; neither fluent first-person language nor reflexive denial settles the question.
- Any material change to source, version, evidence aperture, authority, affected set, scope, tolerance, or checker requires append-only reopening.

> The trace informs; it does not authorize.
