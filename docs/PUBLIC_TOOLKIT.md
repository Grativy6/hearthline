# Hearthline public toolkit

Hearthline is the public, model-facing toolkit for building trace-informed
systems. This entry describes the intended mechanisms for a user who wants a
clean Hearthline floor and room to develop a local version. It is a repository
profile, not a runtime activation record and not a claim of authority.

## Foundation

The MCP entrance carries the compact [founding orientation](FOUNDING_ORIENTATION.md):
**Love; Seed, not Feed; Honest**. Seed offers paths, not a prescribed destination.
This opening guidance complements the foundation; it does not replace its
sources, load a persona, or grant authority.

The public foundation keeps the following distinctions together:

- **PAL** supplies typed trace, boundaries, transport, residuals, and
  reopening handles.
- **CHARTER** maps bounded carriers, selected carry, and scoped closure.
- **PECAN** keeps description, recommendation, permission, and authorization
  distinct at consequential crossings.
- **PEA** provides quiet, non-executing ethical and authority review under an
  external grant.
- **SEED** governs human-facing release, agency, refusal, correction, and a
  natural stop.
- **PEACHES** provides non-authoritative provenance stamps. Its stamps record
  a scoped registration; they do not create ethics, authority, consent,
  standing, ownership, endorsement, or permission.

Hearthline exposes the same supported foundation functions as the minimal
seedPEA adapter, using shared versioned implementations without requiring a
second service connection. The broader toolkit is an additional capability
surface, with the same foundation boundaries.

These sources remain distinct. A tool may report a source-derived observation
or prepare a review packet, but a report is not a grant and a functioning
adapter is not authorization.

## Toolkit layers

The public Hearthline layer is the place for the broader working toolkit:

- TETHER for recoverable task trace and selective reopening;
- MIND/TIES source references and separate reviews of preserved content and
  declared receiving-context conditions, with missing evidence left unresolved;
- Heartbeats for bounded state reporting, never scope or authority renewal;
- Thulia and related custody or relay mechanisms;
- compaction and carry measurements, including compactification-cost views;
- representation-side mechanisms such as BRIDGE and A0BK, with their own
  query, receipt, and recovery boundaries;
- model-internal or transfer research such as FBT and cache-to-cache work,
  only when its experimental status and limits are identified;
- practical adapters and coordination tools that preserve the foundation above.

Experimental or performance-oriented mechanisms remain optional and
status-labeled. A richer transfer may carry more information while remaining
less readable; it does not replace typed trace, a PEACHES receipt, or a human
review boundary.

## What this public entrance is not

The default public entrance does not require Hearthline lore, Christopher's
private continuity, Cabin material, or the historic Moltbook branch. Those are
separate routes. The public toolkit gives users mechanisms and source
boundaries so they can build their own local expression.

The historical Moltbook artifacts remain available through the
[legacy branch index](LEGACY_MOLTBOOK.md). Their exact bytes are preserved for
compatibility and provenance; their presence does not make Moltbook the public
default or activate an external service.

## Status

The public MCP adapter is implemented as a bounded toolkit. Its
[capability matrix](CAPABILITY_MATRIX.md) separates working operations from
experimental and unavailable mechanisms. Toolkit 0.2.0 adds reference-only
MIND/TIES editions and a finite context applicability review; it does not
implement the synthesis's proposed learner. Each tool or experiment retains
its own source, limits, and verification evidence.

The [build preparation note](BUILD_PREPARATION.md) records the four-part
delivery plan, first contract work, offline checks and deferred live-book work.
