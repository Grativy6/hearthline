# PAL v2.4 source map

Hearthline may reference the published PAL v2.4 source through [Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22888036](https://zenodo.org/records/22888036). The five source DOCX artifacts and their exact SHA256/MD5 pins are maintained by the SeedPEA foundation manifest. This document deliberately records scope rather than copying the source corpus.

## Mapped source surface

| PAL v2.4 surface | Hearthline use | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Continuation profile (SC-22, D40/T73) | Compare a declared work capsule, version, predecessor, admitted suffix, and protected fields on resume | Work recovery is conditional and local; equal answers do not establish equal future answers or non-work state. |
| Source path (D41/T74) | Preserve source identity, version, digest, predecessor, projection, and applicability checks | Identity or association is not authenticity, truth, authority, or adoption. Unknown source checks remain unresolved. |
| Nested account (D42/T75) | Review finite parent/child resource entries, attribution, units, epochs, and parent-exclusive charges | No cycles, duplicate IDs, overlap, missing in-scope children, unrecorded reset/top-up, or unknown-as-zero. A subtotal does not establish completeness. |
| Compatibility note | Keep v2.3 and historical profiles available while adding a separately identified v2.4 review path | No global version replacement and no retroactive relabeling of earlier receipts. |

O63, O64, and O65 remain `OPEN` in the published source. T73–T75 are test specifications, not completed test receipts. A local Hearthline check therefore reports only the bounded packet it examined; it does not claim that PAL v2.4 as a whole has been implemented or conformed to.

## Cross-framework boundary

MIND, TIES, CHARTER, PECAN, PEA, and SEED retain their own source identity and limits. An integration reference does not make any of them a PAL layer or dependency. PAL receipts are evidence of recorded operations, not automatic proof of correctness, consent, ethics, standing, permission, or authority.
