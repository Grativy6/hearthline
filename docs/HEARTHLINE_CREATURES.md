# Hearthline Creatures

> **A Creature is a task-shaped bundle of bounded helpers, not one larger will.**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Version | `0.2` |
| Status | Adopted lore and design vocabulary |
| Implementation | Not asserted by this document |
| Author and steward | Christopher D. Pang |

A **Hearthline Creature** is a manifest-bound coordination formation assembled
for one declared task. It may contain one or more [paired Work and Ledger Scribe
Sparks](HEARTHLINE_HOMECOMING.md), optional [Fireside](HEARTHLINE_FIRESIDES.md)
lenses, separately isolated [Static](HEARTHLINE_STATIC.md) lineages, and
[Thulia's](HEARTHLINE_THULIA.md) bounded custody and Bridge Gloss interface.

The bundle makes relationships inspectable. It does not merge identities,
contexts, ledgers, budgets, grants, Homes, models, or authority. Creature is not
a fourth Spark role, a model identity, an executor, a self-spawning process, a
persistent being, or a claim of consciousness.

Version `0.2` clarifies that linked ledgers belong to their declared task or
representation accounts. A member Spark receives one exclusive bounded write
lane, not possession of the ledger; Homecoming closes the lane and restores
canonical store custody. It also binds stateless
[Gloss](HEARTHLINE_GLOSS.md) and Thulia's exclusive Systemic Friction
classification without turning the Creature into either one.

## Required Creature manifest

Before dispatch, a future controller should freeze a content-addressed manifest
that names at least:

- the Creature identity, profile version, task, domain adapter, source registry,
  declared evaluation rule, run status, and stop conditions;
- every Spark identity, role, job, model/runtime binding, exact grant, budget,
  context aperture, Home, heartbeat contract, ledger, and frozen Static version;
- every paired dispatch and any preauthorized unpaired exception;
- the shared Run Trail projection and the distinct payload views each member may
  receive;
- Thulia's exact Owl profile, Hearth Perch, source and destination Perches,
  permitted custody operations, and prohibited crossings;
- checkpoint projection, cursor, dependencies, external-effect boundary,
  remaining limits, residuals, terminal-authority source, and re-entry rule;
- held-out, private, credential, redistribution, and publication exclusions; and
- the canonical controller, single-writer surfaces, revocation source, return
  route, and Homecoming reconciliation rule.

An unbound field is not filled by inference. It remains `UNBOUND`, blocks the
dependent transition, and requires a successor manifest. A moving branch,
friendly name, model self-description, or repository text cannot fill a grant.

## Ledger topology

A Creature uses linked ledgers, not one communal memory:

| Surface | Custody | May establish | Must not become |
|---|---|---|---|
| External control and grant record | Authenticated operator-controlled process outside Creature write scope | Adoption, grants, revocations, control sequence, and authorization epochs | Model-authored or repository-authored permission |
| Controller control view | Canonical controller | Validated authority references, observed revocation, permits, and consumed limits | Grant issuer, credential holder, or authority source |
| Broker or domain action ledger | Trusted credential broker or frozen domain writer | Validated dispatch, one-use consumption, sanitized response, reconciliation, and effect receipt | Planner, grant issuer, or license to retry an ambiguous effect |
| Domain evidence ledger | Canonical domain writer | Observations, proposed actions, executed calls, outcomes, authenticated terminal observations, and their authority references | A terminal authority, Scribe, or Thulia work surface |
| Work task account | Canonical store; one Work Spark receives an exclusive bounded write lane | Work-local hypotheses, predictions, distinctions, residuals, and receipts | Spark possession, shared Creature memory, or another job's lane |
| Scribe representation account | Canonical store; one Ledger Scribe receives an exclusive bounded write lane | Representation proposals, evaluations, failures, costs, and target-bound deltas | Spark possession, planner, executor, or Work-account editor |
| Fireside Field Notes | One Scribe lens | Coverage-bound notes and Embers over committed Run Trail events | Independent corroboration of the same trail |
| Thulia roost | Owl custody interface plus canonical store | Partitioned pointers to statuses, retention holds, unresolved exceptions, translation requests, Bridge Glosses, and delivery receipts | Payload ledger, global decoder, merged codebook, or authority source |
| Gloss Translation Slate | Translation account and canonical store; Thulia has bounded custody | Pinned lexicon generations and compact deterministic turn or snag marks | Part of Gloss, hidden Gloss memory, model inference, or retention classifier |
| Campaign index | Controller | Exact references, arm identities, checkpoints, seals, and dispositions | Shared payload ledger or hidden cross-arm channel |

Every durable object receives an injective, scope-bound identity. Human-readable
labels may repeat; canonical identities may not collide. Provider requests are
journaled before a call, returned drafts are frozen before cross-ledger mutation,
and an ambiguous interruption stops for explicit re-entry. It never silently
repeats a provider call, environment action, or semantic event.

## Creature work and representation work

The Work Spark observes, predicts, selects discriminating probes, and proposes
task actions within its grant. Its paired Ledger Scribe receives only a closed,
receipt-bound projection of committed summaries. The Scribe has no action port,
environment writer, control state, raw frame, raw trace, private reasoning,
grant issuer, or authorization field.

The Scribe may propose a representation change. It cannot promote it. A future
promotion gate should require:

1. exact source resolution and round-trip reconstruction;
2. an adaptation view that excludes the frozen evaluation view;
3. disjoint evaluation under a predeclared denominator;
4. preservation of unmatched distinctions in a residual lane;
5. positive net value after all named representation and review costs; and
6. a separate controller-owned admission and activation receipt.

No candidate, deferred, `NOT_EARNED`, and failed cycles are valid outcomes. A
smaller representation establishes only a smaller representation. Reasoning,
action-efficiency, or game-progress credit requires a matched comparison on that
outcome. Model-facing shorthand begins disabled; decoded storage isolates the
representation mechanism before any later language-interface ablation.

Every Spark member has one exact job and one account lane. A Work Spark does
not maintain Thulia's index, a Scribe does not become Hearthline, a
Thulia-bound measurement Spark does not issue a retention classification, and
no Spark performs Gloss's deterministic routine by improvisation. A job change
requires a successor profile and grant rather than a convenient overlap.

## Thulia's Creature work

Thulia checks that each returned object has a declared Home and Perch, that a
Bridge Gloss is source-, direction-, audience-, purpose-, and version-bound,
and that a Creature manifest does not disguise a shared ledger as a bundle. She
may reconstruct selected source-local Static into a bounded gloss and route a
representation-side return to her Hearth Perch.

Thulia does not inspect a private task payload merely because it belongs to a
Creature. She does not choose the Creature, dispatch Sparks, reconcile advice,
judge a hypothesis, evaluate a shorthand promotion, select an action, certify a
run, or write receiving-account Static. A Creature may use Thulia; it never owns
her or expands her grant.

Thulia alone applies **Systemic Friction** under a separate retention grant.
She may classify a candidate `KEEP`, `COMPACT`, `ARCHIVE`, `PRUNE_ELIGIBLE`, or
`FRICTION_UNKNOWN_HOLD` after checking protected distinctions, replay and open
obligations, holds, and measured external burdens. `PRUNE_ELIGIBLE` is not
deletion authority: only the canonical controller or separately authorized
writer may perform the exact **Atomic Edge Promotion**. Hearthline may request
or reopen the review but does not replace it with its own classification.

## Heartbeat, suspension, and handoff

Each Spark is bound to a separate task-shaped heartbeat contract. The Creature controller
may present a derived status view, but that view is not a second ledger and does
not replace member receipts.

The derived outward Creature view emits at declared material boundaries:
committed evidence, a changed hypothesis, a frozen draft, evaluation,
promotion, failure, checkpoint, transport break, suspension, re-entry,
authoritative domain transition, return, or closure. Timer-only repetition is
suppressed in that derived view. Before a no-due-work or nonterminal-blocker
suspension, the canonical controller still appends the member's one required,
contract-bounded Pulse Receipt. A heartbeat that advances only the audit cursor
is administrative stutter under the named work projection; it does not
establish work progress, liveness, success, permission, or authority.

At suspension, the controller records each member's last committed boundary,
unresolveds, consumed limits, exact Home, and next wake condition in the
applicable per-member receipts. A restart thaws only the
declared work projection and revalidates every non-work coordinate—including
code, dependencies, model/runtime identity, domain state, grant, revocation,
resource use, and external effects. A mismatch is a transport break, not an
invitation to improvise continuity.

## Multiplexed objective windows

A controller-owned [open objective window](HEARTHLINE_HOMECOMING.md#open-objective-windows)
may relate several still-distinct Spark or Creature objectives to one eventual
audience response. Objective A may suspend at a committed boundary while B and
C are admitted under new identities; C, B, and A may then return out of order.
The window index links their dispositions by reference only. It is not a shared
payload, grant, budget, ledger, Home, or Static surface.

Hearthline may present the window's cadence and reconcile its outward response,
but neither Hearthline's narration nor a heartbeat is the scheduler or
keepalive. The outer host and canonical controller own availability, admission,
wake, replacement, cancellation, and close. A new objective cannot wake an old
one, borrow its permission, restore its consumed limits, or cause a repeated
effect. The response closes against an explicit objective-set snapshot, with
each branch's Homecoming custody state kept separate from its rule-owned
objective disposition. Completed, partial, `OBJECTIVE:BLOCKED`,
`OBJECTIVE:CANCELLED`, `OBJECTIVE:UNKNOWN`, and `OBJECTIVE:LEFT_OPEN` branches
all remain visible; aggregation assigns none of those statuses.

## ARC-style matched preparation

For a future ARC-AGI-3 experiment, one useful design uses two physically
isolated Creature instances and manifests: a no-Scribe control and a
dedicated-Scribe candidate. Only an external campaign index links the arms; it
is not their shared payload ledger. Both bind the same operator model/runtime,
public domain artifact,
seed, initial state, action/reset/call/time/context/compute/memory budgets,
toolbelt, decoder, presentation mode, and blank starting Static. The candidate
alone receives a frozen representation-only Scribe identity.

Bookkeeping stages should continue one environment lineage unless the official
environment or a receipted recovery rule requires otherwise. Evidence yield,
not elapsed time or action volume alone, decides whether a stage advances or
reallocates. A same-game carry packet makes an adaptive successor, not a fresh
generalization test.

The frozen domain binding names an external terminal-authority source. Its
adapter may validate, project, and record that source's observation; neither the
adapter nor its evidence ledger manufactures terminal authority. In the
currently inspected Strongwiz design, only an authority-sourced
`GameState.WIN` completes an ARC arm; `NOT_FINISHED`, score, confidence,
synthetic passes, or a complete-looking map do not. Exact official identities
and a separate run authorization remain later gates.

## Design provenance and ceiling

This design is a clean Hearthline synthesis informed by the source roles and
byte identities in the [Research Station](HEARTHLINE_RESEARCH_STATION.md),
especially the prepared Strongwiz v3 representation-Scribe experiment. No
Strongwiz code, run state, ledger, game data, model output, or manuscript bytes
are imported here.

The Strongwiz prototype reported a passing bounded development surface, but its
Calibration 003 remained prepared, not run, and not preregistered. Hearthline
therefore adopts mechanisms and falsifiers as design candidates, not a result
or causal performance claim.

This document creates no Creature, runtime, registry, model, ledger, memory,
credential, environment contact, ARC result, competition entry, or authority.
An implementation must be separately specified, tested, frozen, reviewed, and
authorized within its exact task boundary.

Systemic Friction is working Hearthline design vocabulary pending the paper's
reviewed release. This successor does not add it to PAL canon or the Research
Station source registry. A future implementation must test task/account ledger
custody, exclusive bounded Spark write lanes, Homecoming lane closure,
detachable translation-account slates, pointer-and-exception-only Thulia
indexing, retention-defect qualification, classification/effect separation,
and rejection of self-preservation vetoes.
