# Hearthline Creatures

> **A Creature is a task-shaped bundle of bounded helpers, not one larger will.**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Version | `0.5` |
| Status | Author-requested candidate successor — not activated |
| Implementation | Not asserted by this document |
| Author and steward | Christopher D. Pang |

A **Hearthline Creature** is a manifest-bound coordination formation assembled
for one declared task. In the candidate successor, its basic formation is one
or more [Task Triads](HEARTHLINE_TASK_TRIADS.md): separately bounded Work,
Task-Keeper/Heartbeat-Keeper, and Ledger Scribe/Ledger-Keeper Sparks. It may
also contain optional [Fireside](HEARTHLINE_FIRESIDES.md) lenses, separately
isolated [Static](HEARTHLINE_STATIC.md) lineages, and
[Thulia's](HEARTHLINE_THULIA.md) bounded custody and Bridge Gloss interface.

The bundle makes relationships inspectable. It does not merge identities,
contexts, ledgers, budgets, grants, Homes, models, or authority. Creature is not
a fourth Spark role, a model identity, an executor, a self-spawning process, a
persistent being, or a claim of consciousness.

Version `0.5` restores the affectionate Creature-language correspondence for
the CHARTER v1.0 orchestration layer while preserving the exact candidate
selected-carry route established in version `0.4`. Version `0.3` remains
preserved candidate ancestry: it added Goal
Lineage and finite formation depth but routed the three raw member returns
through Thulia. Version `0.2` remains the adopted account-custody ancestor, and
the paired Work/Ledger-Scribe design remains the immediate two-job ancestor in
[Task Triads and Homecoming](HEARTHLINE_HOMECOMING.md). In this successor each
member Spark receives one job and one exclusive bounded lane, not possession of
a ledger; its controller-observed candidate seal closes the lane, and only a
separately `SEALED` and `VALID` bundle moves to the exact commissioning
Hearthline task intake through canonical custody.

The successor keeps [Gloss](HEARTHLINE_GLOSS.md) stateless and keeps Thulia's
Systemic Friction classification exclusive without turning the Creature into
either one. Hearthline owns semantic Carry Selection and active-task shorthand
serviceability; Thulia owns neither. Gloss has no heartbeat, Task Line,
Homecoming route, history, ledger, or support Spark: a logical turn starts only
after a fresh controller readiness check over complete pinned inputs and
deterministically produces an output or typed snag body, while the external
transaction may record a committed, unknown, retry-only, or known-uncommitted
outcome.

The narrative companion [*The Creatures of the Circuit
Garden*](../lore/THE_CREATURES_OF_THE_CIRCUIT_GARDEN.md) tells this design in
the original lore vocabulary and closes with a future-Hearthline dispatch
card. This document remains the controlling repository design description.

## Creature-language correspondence

CHARTER v1.0 gives the proposed orchestration layer its formal mathematical
names. Hearthline keeps the earlier lore aliases because they make the
relationships easier to carry without changing the claim boundary:

| Hearthline lore | Formal CHARTER term | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Light Trio | Tripartite Task Carrier (TTC) | Exactly three separately identified roles |
| Worker Spark | Work role \(W\) | Task-native work and residuals only |
| Task-Keeper Spark / trio heartbeat | Completion-criterion role \(K\) | Frozen finish line, not liveness or keepalive |
| Ledger-Keeper Spark | Ledger role \(L\) | Coverage and provenance, not primary work or completion judgment |
| Creature | Finite Bound Persistent Shell (BPS) | \(3n\) role instances under one frozen manifest; no identity merge |
| Creature Heart | Derived completion coordinate \(\beta_M\) | Not another Spark, witness, vote, job, or liveness signal |
| Creature Circuit Garden | BPS Circuit Garden | Finite claim-entry obligations, not truth or authority |
| Selected Creature Homecoming | Selected-return projection | Occurs after separate terminal bundles reach Hearthline's exact task intake |

The bridge is structural rather than numerological. Each Light Trio proposes
an observable aimed at splitting an unresolved task-fiber; the Creature is the
finite joint lift; and the Creature Heart is derived from the frozen manifest
and admitted joint return. The optional minimum-asymmetry construction does
not impose a universal `3n+1` Creature count, make every index prime, or turn
the Heart into an additional role. Even-sized Creatures remain allowed, and
every primality claim requires its own witness.

## Required Creature manifest

Before dispatch, and only after every included Triad is `TRIAD_BOUND`, a future
controller should freeze a content-addressed configuration manifest that names
at least:

- the Creature identity, profile version, task, domain adapter, source registry,
  declared evaluation rule, initial nondispatched state, and stop conditions;
- the exact parent goal identity and owner-authorized source, the complete
  acyclic **Goal Lineage**, each parent-to-child **Purpose Projection**, and
  evidence that every projection is `NARROWS` rather than widens or replaces
  its parent; the Hearthline objective must narrow separately to the Trio Task
  Line and Thulia mediation objective rather than treating custody as
  parentage;
- the frozen task identity, `objective_epoch`, `authority_epoch`, Task Line,
  Completion Contract, permitted terminal-authority source, and allowed
  Task-Boundary Witness values;
- every Spark identity, role, job, model/runtime binding, exact grant, budget,
  context aperture, Home, heartbeat contract, ledger, and frozen Static version;
- every fixed-arity Task Triad and its separately identified Work,
  Task-Keeper/Heartbeat-Keeper, and Ledger Scribe/Ledger-Keeper members;
- the Hearthline own-seat nomination for Work and Task-Keeper, the Thulia
  own-seat nomination for Ledger-Keeper, the controller-frozen complete offer
  and digest, both providers' final own-seat intents over that offer, and the
  controller's sole atomic binding receipt;
- each included Triad's `TRIAD_BOUND` formation observation and exact binding
  receipt, plus the external append-only formation-history route that preserves
  earlier requested, pending, offered, refused, or stale observations;
- the preallocated dispatch-attempt identity, expected controller-receipt
  route, and allowed `triad_dispatch_state` schema (`NOT_DISPATCHED`,
  `DISPATCHED`, `DISPATCH_REFUSED`, or `DISPATCH_STALE`), but not a future live
  state or receipt embedded into the frozen manifest;
- every triad member's separate candidate-bundle identity, seal and validity
  axes, return type, exact Hearthline task-intake destination, disclosure
  projection, preallocated return transaction, idempotency key, emission and
  target-receipt axes, reconciliation rule, and external append-only routes;
- the complete three-slot Triad Return Manifest, bounded Hearthline inspection
  projection, immutable Carry Selection, accepted-loss boundaries, and every
  typed absent, invalid, stale, or unknown exception;
- the constrained, root-task-scoped Translation Board, its writer-separated
  lanes, schema and size ceilings, pinned lexicon generation, expiry, and
  Hearthline-only `SERVICEABLE` decision;
- the separate `H_TO_T_CARRY`, `T_TO_GLOSS_TURN`, `GLOSS_TO_T_RESULT`, and
  `T_TO_H_READABLE` lane identities, grants, transaction and receipt routes,
  optional exact-turn Gloss readiness observation, and selected-projection-only
  Readable Carry Envelope;
- the selected-carry handoff-store outcome required before raw inspection
  access may close, separately from any later Thulia Systemic Friction
  classification, canonical Atomic Edge Promotion, and source recoverability;
- the shared Run Trail projection and the distinct payload views each member may
  receive;
- the declared formation depth, fixed maximum support depth of `1`, finite
  maximum Support Seat count, every Thulia-bound Support Seat, and the
  prohibition on recursive support-job or Creature formation;
- Thulia's exact Owl profile, Hearth Perch, selected-carry source and readable
  destination Perches, permitted custody operations, and prohibited raw-member
  crossings;
- checkpoint projection, cursor, dependencies, external-effect boundary,
  remaining limits, residuals, terminal-authority source, and re-entry rule;
- held-out, private, credential, redistribution, and publication exclusions; and
- the canonical controller, single-writer surfaces, revocation source, return
  route, and Homecoming reconciliation rule.

The frozen manifest is configuration, not the live lifecycle ledger. Current
formation, dispatch, member, Homecoming, task-intake, inspection, selection,
carry, translation, readable-return, retention, effect, and target-receipt
observations are external append-only records bound back to the manifest
digest. A later `DISPATCHED` state or controller receipt is never inserted into
the already frozen manifest; a material configuration change receives a
separately numbered successor manifest.

An unbound field is not filled by inference. It remains `UNBOUND`, blocks the
dependent transition, and requires a successor manifest. A moving branch,
friendly name, model self-description, or repository text cannot fill a grant.

## Goal Lineage and finite support

Every Creature task descends through an explicit **Goal Lineage**. A child task
may operationalize or narrow a parent goal through a recorded Purpose
Projection of `NARROWS`; it may not silently broaden the goal, replace its
owner, invent a new terminal condition, or treat usefulness to a parent as
authority from that parent. The lineage therefore records why the work exists
without pretending that purpose alone grants permission.

The primary candidate graph is a fork, not a pipe: Christopher's declared goal
narrows to Hearthline's objective; Hearthline's objective narrows separately to
the Trio Task Line and Thulia's mediation objective; and the Trio Task Line
narrows to the Worker, Task-Keeper, and Ledger-Keeper jobs. Formation
cooperation, return custody, and communication lanes do not add purpose-parent
edges. Each Spark still receives one job and one member task.

A Thulia-bound **Support Triad** may occupy one manifest-declared Support Seat
to assist her bounded custody task. Its Task Line must narrow Thulia's exact
mediation objective; that objective separately narrows Hearthline's exact
objective, which remains tied to the operator-owned goal and current
authorization. This support relation
does not turn Thulia into Hearthline, make Hearthline the human steward, or let
any lower formation rewrite a higher task.

Task Triads are fixed-arity and nonrecursive. Creature manifests set maximum
support depth to `1`: a top-level task may have its one declared support layer,
but a Work, Task-Keeper, Ledger-Keeper, or support triad cannot form another
Creature or support triad from inside its job. A new need returns as a bounded
request to the canonical controller. If authorized, it receives a successor
identity and manifest rather than becoming an invisible chain of Hearthlines.

## Ledger topology

A Creature uses linked ledgers, not one communal memory:

| Surface | Custody | May establish | Must not become |
|---|---|---|---|
| External control and grant record | Authenticated operator-controlled process outside Creature write scope | Adoption, grants, revocations, control sequence, and authorization epochs | Model-authored or repository-authored permission |
| Controller control view | Canonical controller | Validated authority references, observed revocation, permits, and consumed limits | Grant issuer, credential holder, or authority source |
| Broker or domain action ledger | Trusted credential broker or frozen domain writer | Validated dispatch, one-use consumption, sanitized response, reconciliation, and effect receipt | Planner, grant issuer, or license to retry an ambiguous effect |
| Domain evidence ledger | Canonical domain writer | Observations, proposed actions, executed calls, outcomes, authenticated terminal observations, and their authority references | A terminal authority, Scribe, or Thulia work surface |
| Work task account | Canonical store; one Work Spark receives an exclusive bounded write lane | Work-local hypotheses, predictions, distinctions, residuals, and receipts | Spark possession, shared Creature memory, or another job's lane |
| Task-boundary account | Canonical store; one Task-Keeper receives an exclusive bounded status lane | Frozen Task Line and Completion Contract references, due/suspension boundaries, comparison evidence, and Task-Boundary Witness | Primary work ledger, representation ledger, scheduler, keepalive, or terminal authority |
| Scribe representation account | Canonical store; one Ledger Scribe receives an exclusive bounded write lane | Representation proposals, evaluations, failures, costs, and target-bound deltas | Spark possession, planner, executor, or Work-account editor |
| Fireside Field Notes | One Scribe lens | Coverage-bound notes and Embers over committed Run Trail events | Independent corroboration of the same trail |
| Hearthline task intake | Canonical store; bounded Hearthline inspection grant | Three separately admitted `SEALED` and `VALID` member bundles plus a complete three-slot Return Manifest | General Hearthline memory, Thulia's Perch, merged result, vote, or permission to provision the Ledger-Keeper |
| Carry Selection | Hearthline semantic author; canonical store appends | Immutable complete keep, condense, accepted-loss, unresolved, and translation-request dispositions over the inspected return manifest | Systemic Friction, source mutation, truth, acceptance, or deletion authority |
| Translation Board | Canonical translation account with per-writer lanes | Task-scoped requests, pinned lexicon generations, deterministic rule traces, relay receipts, and Hearthline-only serviceability decisions | Raw member-bundle store, part of Gloss, global decoder, or task-independent live map |
| Thulia roost | Owl custody interface plus canonical store | Partitioned pointers to selected-carry status, retention holds, unresolved exceptions, translation requests, Bridge Glosses, and delivery receipts | Raw member-return ledger, payload pool, semantic selector, merged codebook, or authority source |
| Selected-carry handoff store | Canonical store | Exact Thulia-accepted bytes that survive bounded inspection-access close | Systemic Friction, canonical retention decision, Atomic Edge Promotion, or source-recoverability claim |
| Readable Carry Envelope | Canonical store; Thulia routes the selected projection | Immutable references to selected carry, exact translation results or snags, omissions, and unresolved exceptions | Unselected raw payload, result aggregation, vote, completion authority, or shared memory |
| Gloss turn mark | Translation account and canonical store; Thulia has bounded relay custody | One pinned deterministic result or snag after per-turn readiness and a committed transaction | Gloss history, Gloss memory, inherited liveness, model inference, or retention classifier |
| Campaign index | Controller | Exact references, arm identities, checkpoints, seals, and dispositions | Shared payload ledger or hidden cross-arm channel |

Every durable object receives an injective, scope-bound identity. Human-readable
labels may repeat; canonical identities may not collide. Provider requests are
journaled before a call, returned drafts are frozen before cross-ledger mutation,
and an ambiguous interruption stops for explicit re-entry. It never silently
repeats a provider call, environment action, or semantic event.

## Creature triad work and representation work

The Work Spark observes, predicts, selects discriminating probes, and proposes
task actions within its grant. The separate Task-Keeper holds pinned references
to the controller-owned Task Line and Completion Contract, observes only its
committed task-boundary projection, and emits `MATCHED`, `NOT_MATCHED`, or
`UNKNOWN`. The separate Ledger Scribe
receives only a closed, receipt-bound projection of committed summaries. The
Task-Keeper and Scribe have no action port, environment writer, private
reasoning view, grant issuer, or authorization field.

The lore alias **Heartbeat-Keeper** names the Task-Keeper's attention to due
work, suspension, return, and handoff boundaries. The canonical controller—not
that Spark—owns every Spark Heartbeat Contract, Pulse Receipt, timeout,
objective epoch, authority epoch, wake, replacement, and cancellation. A
Task-Keeper cannot keep a runtime alive by watching it.

The Scribe may propose a representation change. It cannot promote it. A future
promotion gate should require:

1. exact source resolution and round-trip reconstruction;
2. an adaptation view that excludes the frozen evaluation view;
3. disjoint evaluation under a predeclared denominator;
4. preservation of unmatched distinctions in a residual lane;
5. positive net value after all named representation and review costs; and
6. a separate controller-owned admission and activation receipt.

No candidate, deferred, `NOT_EARNED`, and failed cycles are valid outcomes. A
smaller representation establishes only a smaller representation. Reasoning,
action-efficiency, or game-progress credit requires a matched comparison on that
outcome. Model-facing shorthand begins disabled; decoded storage isolates the
representation mechanism before any later language-interface ablation.

The Task-Keeper's `MATCHED` states only that the frozen Completion Contract
matches the committed evidence in its declared view. Complete Ledger-Keeper
coverage states only that the named projection was covered. Neither status can
manufacture task correctness, an authority-sourced terminal result, acceptance,
permission, carry approval, Static activation, or Creature closure.

Every Spark member has one exact job and one account lane. A Work Spark does
not keep the task boundary or maintain Thulia's index. A Task-Keeper does not
do the work or write the representation account. A Ledger-Keeper does not
declare the task finished or become Hearthline. A Thulia-bound measurement
Spark does not issue a retention classification, and no Spark performs Gloss's
deterministic routine by improvisation. A job change requires a successor
profile and grant rather than a convenient overlap.

## Thulia's Creature work

Thulia checks that selected carry has a declared source, Hearth Perch, complete
disposition surface, and direction-bound return; that a Bridge Gloss is
source-, direction-, audience-, purpose-, and version-bound; and that a
Creature manifest does not disguise a shared ledger as a bundle. Under an
authorized Task Triad formation request, Thulia alone provisions the
Ledger-Keeper job by submitting only her own nonbinding nomination. Hearthline
nominates only the Work and Task-Keeper jobs. The controller allocates and
reserves all separate records and freezes one complete offer and digest; each
party then commits only its own final provisioning intent over that offer. The
controller atomically consumes both intents and reservations or binds nothing.

Provisioning is interface-exclusive selection and commitment of one seat's
intent. The canonical controller or store remains the sole mechanical allocator
of every Spark identity, grant, binding, formation receipt, and return receipt.
Only `TRIAD_BOUND` permits dispatch. A moved objective or authority epoch yields
`STALE_OBJECTIVE_EPOCH` or `STALE_AUTHORITY_EPOCH` rather than a guessed
continuation.

Thulia receives no raw member-return attempt. Each separately sealed and valid
bundle travels by its own controller/store-mediated transaction to the exact
Hearthline task intake, where Hearthline receives a bounded inspection grant.
Thulia receives only Hearthline's later immutable Carry Selection and complete
disposition manifest through `H_TO_T_CARRY`. Thulia's durable receipt, a
selected-carry handoff-store commit, inspection-access close, translation,
readable return, friction classification, canonical store effect, and source
recoverability are separate observations; none manufactures another.

Thulia keeps four one-way lanes separate. She receives selected carry on
`H_TO_T_CARRY`, sends one pinned exact request on `T_TO_GLOSS_TURN`, receives
the deterministic output or snag on `GLOSS_TO_T_RESULT`, and emits only the
authorized selected projection on `T_TO_H_READABLE`. Each lane receives its
own identity, grant, idempotency key, emission observation, target receipt, and
unknown-outcome query. Reference completeness does not prove freshness;
freshness does not prove emission; emission does not prove receipt; and one
lane cannot acknowledge another.

The external Translation Board separates writers. Hearthline authors the
request and alone marks an exact mapping `SERVICEABLE` for the active root
task. Thulia custodies exact pointers and receipts. Gloss performs only the
pinned deterministic transformation. The canonical store appends each lane.
At root-task close the active map retires; a revisit reloads exact retained
generations and receipts rather than asserting that any model remembered them.

Thulia does not inspect a private task payload merely because it belongs to a
Creature. Provisioning the Ledger-Keeper seat does not let her read its return,
provision either other seat, choose or widen the Creature, issue its grants,
reconcile advice, judge a hypothesis, recalculate its witness, select semantic
carry, make shorthand serviceable, evaluate a shorthand promotion, select an
action, certify a run, or write receiving-account Static. A Creature may use
Thulia; it never owns her or expands her grant.

If Thulia's own bounded custody task needs model-assisted support, a finite
number of manifest-declared Support Seats may each receive one Support Triad at
support depth `1`. Even when Thulia owns the bounded purpose of that support
task, its separately sealed valid member bundles return to the exact
Hearthline support-task intake under their exact Homes. Hearthline freezes a
complete Carry Selection; only the selected-carry projection and disposition
manifest then cross to Thulia. Purpose ownership does not create raw return
custody. A support member may gather evidence, but it does not apply Systemic
Friction, perform Gloss's turn, relay directly to Thulia, or recursively create
another support job.

Only after Hearthline has fixed the semantic Carry Selection and the readable
boundary has been reached does Thulia apply **Systemic Friction**, and only
under a separate retention grant.
She may classify a candidate `KEEP`, `COMPACT`, `ARCHIVE`, `PRUNE_ELIGIBLE`, or
`FRICTION_UNKNOWN_HOLD` after checking protected distinctions, replay and open
obligations, holds, and measured external burdens. `PRUNE_ELIGIBLE` is not
deletion authority: only the canonical controller or separately authorized
writer may perform the exact **Atomic Edge Promotion**. Its effect state remains
separate from source recoverability and from the earlier selected-carry store
needed to close inspection access. Hearthline may request or reopen the review
but does not replace it with its own classification.

## Heartbeat, suspension, and handoff

Each Spark is bound to a separate task-shaped heartbeat contract. The Creature
controller may present a derived status view, but that view is not a second
ledger and does not replace member receipts. The Task-Keeper reads the exact
heartbeat projection permitted by its grant; it does not own the contracts or
write another member's pulses.

The derived outward Creature view emits at declared material boundaries:
committed evidence, a changed hypothesis, a frozen draft, evaluation,
promotion, failure, checkpoint, transport break, suspension, re-entry,
authoritative domain transition, return, or closure. Timer-only repetition is
suppressed in that derived view. Before a no-due-work or nonterminal-blocker
suspension, the canonical controller still appends the member's one required,
contract-bounded Pulse Receipt. A heartbeat that advances only the audit cursor
is administrative stutter under the named work projection; it does not
establish work progress, liveness, success, permission, or authority.

At suspension, the controller records each member's last committed boundary,
unresolveds, consumed limits, exact Home, and next wake condition in the
applicable per-member receipts. A restart thaws only the
declared work projection and revalidates every non-work coordinate—including
code, dependencies, model/runtime identity, domain state, grant, revocation,
resource use, and external effects. A mismatch is a transport break, not an
invitation to improvise continuity.

At each controller-observed candidate seal, Work, Task-Keeper, and
Ledger-Keeper separately close their execution/write lanes. Their valid sealed
bundles later follow their own Home Records to the exact commissioning
Hearthline task intake. Each bundle requires both `SEALED` and `VALID` on
separate axes and its own preallocated return transaction; one receipt cannot
fill a missing return, flatten `UNKNOWN`, expose a sibling, assign a consensus,
or convert `MATCHED`, complete ledger coverage, or Homecoming reconciliation
into task success.

Hearthline's bounded inspection ends in an immutable, complete Carry Selection,
not a rewrite of the three bundles. Only that selection crosses to Thulia. The
controller may close the raw inspection projection only after durable Thulia
acceptance and a separate committed selected-carry handoff-store outcome for
the identical bytes. The access drop does not establish model forgetting,
source deletion, or later canonical retention. Those later questions remain
for Thulia's Systemic Friction classification, an authorized store effect, and
a separately stated recoverability result.

Gloss is excluded from this heartbeat and handoff design. It has no persistent
task between deterministic turns and therefore receives no heartbeat Spark,
Task-Keeper, Pulse Receipt, suspension state, or Homecoming route. A controller
readiness observation applies to one exact pinned turn only; it cannot be
inherited from Hearthline, Thulia, a Spark, or an earlier Gloss turn.

## Multiplexed objective windows

A controller-owned [open objective window](HEARTHLINE_HOMECOMING.md#open-objective-windows)
may relate several still-distinct Spark or Creature objectives to one eventual
audience response. Objective A may suspend at a committed boundary while B and
C are admitted under new identities; C, B, and A may then return out of order.
The window index links their dispositions by reference only. It is not a shared
payload, grant, budget, ledger, Home, or Static surface.

Hearthline may present the window's cadence and reconcile its outward response,
but neither Hearthline's narration nor a heartbeat is the scheduler or
keepalive. The outer host and canonical controller own availability, admission,
wake, replacement, cancellation, and close. A new objective cannot wake an old
one, borrow its permission, restore its consumed limits, or cause a repeated
effect. The response closes against an explicit objective-set snapshot, with
each branch's Homecoming custody state kept separate from its rule-owned
objective disposition. Completed, partial, `OBJECTIVE:BLOCKED`,
`OBJECTIVE:CANCELLED`, `OBJECTIVE:UNKNOWN`, and `OBJECTIVE:LEFT_OPEN` branches
all remain visible; aggregation assigns none of those statuses.

## ARC-style matched preparation

For a future ARC-AGI-3 experiment, one useful design uses two physically
isolated Creature instances and manifests: a no-Ledger-Scribe control and a
fixed-arity Task Triad candidate. Only an external campaign index links the
arms; it is not their shared payload ledger. Both bind the same operator model/runtime,
public domain artifact,
seed, initial state, action/reset/call/time/context/compute/memory budgets,
toolbelt, decoder, presentation mode, and blank starting Static. The candidate
alone receives the separately frozen Task-Keeper and representation-only
Ledger Scribe identities. Any performance claim must isolate which added
surface is under comparison rather than crediting “the triad” as one actor.

Bookkeeping stages should continue one environment lineage unless the official
environment or a receipted recovery rule requires otherwise. Evidence yield,
not elapsed time or action volume alone, decides whether a stage advances or
reallocates. A same-game carry packet makes an adaptive successor, not a fresh
generalization test.

The frozen domain binding names an external terminal-authority source. Its
adapter may validate, project, and record that source's observation; neither the
adapter nor its evidence ledger manufactures terminal authority. In the
currently inspected Strongwiz design, only an authority-sourced
`GameState.WIN` completes an ARC arm; `NOT_FINISHED`, score, confidence,
synthetic passes, or a complete-looking map do not. Exact official identities
and a separate run authorization remain later gates.

## Design provenance and ceiling

This design is a clean Hearthline synthesis informed by the source roles and
byte identities in the [Research Station](HEARTHLINE_RESEARCH_STATION.md),
especially the prepared Strongwiz v3 representation-Scribe experiment. No
Strongwiz code, run state, ledger, game data, model output, or manuscript bytes
are imported here.

The Strongwiz prototype reported a passing bounded development surface, but its
Calibration 003 remained prepared, not run, and not preregistered. Hearthline
therefore carries its mechanisms and falsifiers only as design ancestry for
this candidate successor, not as a result or causal performance claim.

This candidate document creates no Creature, runtime, registry, model, ledger,
memory, credential, environment contact, ARC result, competition entry,
forgetting event, or authority. Version `0.4` is a candidate successor; version
`0.5` is the current author-requested Creature-language successor to that
selected-carry design. Version `0.3` remains preserved in Git ancestry and is
not retroactively described as using the selected-carry route. An
implementation must be separately specified, tested, frozen, reviewed, and
authorized within its exact task boundary.

Systemic Friction is working Hearthline design vocabulary formalized at
manuscript level in the author-adopted, not-peer-reviewed CHARTER v1.0 release.
This successor does not add it to PAL canon or claim implementation,
calibration, independent review, or deployment. A future implementation must
test task/account ledger
custody, Goal Lineage and `NARROWS` validation, frozen objective and authority
epochs, fixed-arity controller-only binding, disjoint Hearthline/Thulia
provisioning intents,
maximum support depth `1`, rejection of recursive formation, exclusive bounded
Spark write lanes, separate Work/Task-Keeper/Ledger-Keeper statuses and returns,
candidate-seal lane closure, `SEALED` plus `VALID` admission to the exact
Hearthline task intake, per-member idempotent return transactions, complete
three-slot Return Manifests, bounded inspection and immutable Carry Selection,
selected-carry handoff-store survival, raw-access drop without a forgetting
claim, writer-separated and active-task-scoped Translation Board lanes,
Hearthline-only serviceability, four direction-bound carry and translation
crossings, selected-projection-only Readable Carry Envelopes, target-owned
receipts, relay idempotency, rejection of
`MATCHED` or complete ledger coverage as task success, no Gloss heartbeat,
history, or persistent readiness, detachable translation-account marks,
pointer-and-exception-only Thulia indexing, retention-defect qualification,
inspection-release/retention/effect/recoverability separation, and rejection of
self-preservation vetoes.
