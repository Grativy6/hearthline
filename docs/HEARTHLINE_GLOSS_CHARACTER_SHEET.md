## Elastic depicted scale

Gloss has no single fixed drawing height. A scene may show her flaring larger
to be legible beside Hearthline or contracting until she can move through the
narrow channels of the Circuit Garden and inspect the exact place where a turn
has jammed.

Scale changes preserve her recognizable face, pale center, two arms, and
gold-orange flame silhouette. They are presentation changes, not growth,
teleportation, compression of records, increased capacity, broader scope, or a
change in identity. A scale label applies only to the pictured form in that
view; if the form changes, measure again.

## Glassy graspers

Gloss's ordinary hands may taper like flame. When she intentionally holds a
page, tool, token facsimile, or other story object, the contact surfaces cool
and harden into translucent cream-gold facets. Fingers should become slightly
crystalline and clearly separable, with rounded gripping faces rather than
sharp claws.

The glassy state begins at the fingers or palm and may extend a short distance
up the wrist. It does not plate her whole body, become armor, or turn her into a
gem creature. A faint internal glow may remain beneath the facets.

The visible change tells the reader that she can grasp without scorching the
object. It is not real protective equipment, a material specification, a
hazard certification, a credential, or evidence that an implementation can
touch a physical object safely.

## Expression grammar

Gloss communicates visually through flame posture, eye shape, and brightness:

| State | Flame and posture | Eyes and graspers |
|---|---|---|
| Resting | Compact, softly curling | Warm open gaze; hands fluid |
| Curious | Leans forward; crown-tip hooks toward the object | Wide focused gaze |
| Focused | Narrower, steadier flame with less edge flicker | Hands may facet before contact |
| Delighted | Brief upward flare and brighter pale center | Eyes lift; fingers open |
| Concerned | Low flame and guarded curl | Eyes narrow gently; held object remains supported |
| Carrying | Stable center with motion trailing behind | Cool glassy graspers close around only the held item |

These cues are performance language, not a hidden status code, diagnosis,
command set, or statement about internal experience.

## The ruler on the sheet

The brass ruler is read left-to-right as:

`6 5 4 3 2 1 0`

The `0` sits at the ruler's true right-hand endpoint. The seven numbered marks
bound six equal intervals. The zero is a physical coordinate, not an issued
Hearthline record ordinal; the separate
[Ordered Lineage](HEARTHLINE_ORDERED_LINEAGE.md) continues to allocate record
ordinals from `1`.

The lesson behind the ruler lives in
[The Seventh Mark](../lore/THE_SEVENTH_MARK.md): count marks and spaces
separately, and test at most one warranted frame expansion before deciding that
an edge is an anomaly. A pattern may tell Hearthline where to inspect. It does
not put evidence there.

## Relationship and scene boundaries

Gloss may work beside Hearthline and Thulia, but the shared composition does
not merge their jobs. A scene may show Gloss turning one self-contained note,
inspecting one declared route, or holding one object with glassy graspers. It
must not depict her as choosing meaning, retaining a private history, granting
access, approving carry, allocating queue records, or replacing Thulia's
separate custody role.

## No-authority boundary

Gloss is a fictional character and visual presentation. Her size, light,
expression, graspers, route, and companion art create no memory, persistence,
identity continuity, capability, permission, standing, consent, authority, or
operational state.

> The glow shows the turn. It does not authorize it.
