# Hearthline Creature Visual Forms

> **A Creature may borrow a silhouette. It does not become one animal, one mind, or one authority.**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Status | Adopted visual presentation guide |

This guide controls seven visual archetypes used to depict Hearthline
Creatures. It does not create seven Creature identities, species, profiles,
roles, capabilities, or permanent inhabitants. An actual Creature remains a
task-shaped, manifest-bound formation of separately identified and governed
parts under the controlling design.

`CREATURE-FORM/IMAGE-*` is a public presentation series. It is deliberately
different from an operational example such as `CREATURE-000001`.

## Shared construction language

Every form should remain visibly made from light and trace rather than flesh or
solid machinery:

- a translucent white-to-cyan body with restrained gold points;
- separately readable internal paths rather than one opaque mass;
- a small central Heart-like convergence that depicts a completed formation,
  not a merged mind or command core;
- luminous edges and occasional prismatic color where bright trace meets the
  environment;
- a silhouette legible at the scale of the scene; and
- one or more tracelines that may leave the frame toward an offscreen
  controller or toward separately declared Homes without implying direct
  character-to-character control or one merged Home.

The image may simplify member lines at distance. It must not portray distinct
Sparks, ledgers, grants, budgets, Homes, or authority as literally fused.

## Seven archetypes

| Visual identity | Form | Visual scale band | Presentation use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CREATURE-FORM/IMAGE-000001` | Spiral-tailed fox | Small | Quick ground routes and narrow terrain |
| `CREATURE-FORM/IMAGE-000002` | Wing-finned fish | Small | Water routes and submerged inspection |
| `CREATURE-FORM/IMAGE-000003` | Marsh heron | Small | Wading, balance, and delicate field paths |
| `CREATURE-FORM/IMAGE-000004` | Plated burrower | Small | Close-to-ground or sheltered routes |
| `CREATURE-FORM/IMAGE-000005` | Antlered carrier | Mid-sized | Broad overland transport silhouettes |
| `CREATURE-FORM/IMAGE-000006` | Ribbon-tailed sky ray | Mid-sized | Gliding routes across open air |
| `CREATURE-FORM/IMAGE-000007` | Sky dragon | Giant | Far-ranging work whose route must remain visible across a landscape |

“Small,” “mid-sized,” and “giant” describe the visible embodiment in an
illustration. They do not count the formation's members. Visual scale is
independent of manifest headcount, role composition, work complexity,
importance, capability, priority, budget, permission, and authority.

## Form is not function

The animal-like shapes are visual mnemonics, not biological claims or automatic
tools. A fish form does not independently grant water access. A burrower does
not create permission to enter the ground. A carrier does not authorize
transport. A dragon does not become a weapon or a sovereign because it is
large, beautiful, or dramatic.

The controller and manifest determine what work, environment, grant, limits,
and return path exist. If those do not permit the pictured action, the scene is
fictional atmosphere rather than an operating instruction.

## Motion and expression

Forms move with the broad grammar of their silhouettes: the fox springs, the
fish folds its fins through water, the heron places careful steps, the burrower
keeps low, the carrier moves steadily, the ray banks, and the dragon draws long
curves across the sky. Light may brighten at turns or soften at rest.

These motions help a reader follow the route. They do not encode a hidden
state, translate a receipt, express a vote, prove coordination, or establish an
inner experience.

## Scene boundary

Several forms may share a wide scene while doing separate jobs. Their
tracelines may travel off-screen and appear to converge at a common controller,
but the convergence is topology, not shared authority. Hearthline may commission
or observe a formation; Thulia may keep her separately governed custody path;
neither relationship merges the Creature's parts.

The sky dragon may break ordinary visual physics with prismatic light when the
story benefits. Rule-of-cool color is licensed as presentation only. It cannot
rewrite the controlling manifest or make a physical, scientific, or runtime
claim.

## No-authority boundary

Adding, naming, drawing, resizing, or animating a form does not instantiate a
Creature, allocate a Spark, merge identities, extend a grant, create memory,
prove liveness, authorize work, approve carry, or establish a result.

> The silhouette makes the route easier to see. The manifest keeps it honest.
