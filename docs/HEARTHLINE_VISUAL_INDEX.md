This index preserves the ordered visual development of Hearthline and Thulia. The image ordinals in this first public index are **retrospective archival registrations** assigned to the already-created files before their repository publication. They preserve their observed generation order without claiming that canonical numbers were preallocated before generation. Future visual records should receive the next number in their declared series before repository publication.

A correction or successor receives a new number; it does not replace, renumber, or silently repair an earlier image. Image ordinals record allocation within one declared visual series only. They do not establish chronology outside that series, quality, truth, canonical status, identity continuity, personhood, capability, permission, or authority. An `IMAGE` ordinal also does not advance or replace an Owl `PROFILE` or `SHEET` ordinal.

## Control order

Visual material is interpreted in this order:

1. [Thulia, Hearthline's Owl Scribe](HEARTHLINE_THULIA.md) controls Thulia's role and behavioral boundaries.
2. [Thulia Character Sheet, successor `0.2`](HEARTHLINE_THULIA_CHARACTER_SHEET_000002.md), identity `OWL-000001/SHEET-000002`, controls her current appearance and animation construction.
3. The latest compatible image may illustrate those written rules.
4. Earlier studies and superseded corrections remain provenance.
5. Scene illustrations show narrative relationships, not orthographic geometry.

No image independently controls behavior, access, Static custody, Bridge Gloss handling, activation, or authority. If an image conflicts with the controlling written design, the written design governs and the conflict remains recorded as a residual.

## Status vocabulary

- `REFERENCE` — a supplied visual source or presentation anchor.
- `STUDY` — a preserved, non-controlling visual exploration.
- `SUPERSEDED_STUDY` — an intermediate image retained after a later correction.
- `CURRENT_VISUAL_REFERENCE` — the closest current visual aid, still subordinate to the controlling written sheet.
- `SCENE` — a narrative illustration rather than a construction reference.

“Current” here describes visual usefulness only. It is not runtime adoption or activation.
