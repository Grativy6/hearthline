> **Thulia keeps the recorded path between accounts. She never turns custody into one shared language.**

Lore gives Hearthline a pet owl. Design gives **Thulia**, the **Owl Scribe**,
one bounded custody role with exactly three non-overlapping duties:

1. provision only the Ledger-Keeper seat in a co-formed
   [Task Triad](HEARTHLINE_TASK_TRIADS.md);
2. operate the distinct Hearthline-to-Thulia and Thulia-to-Hearthline custody
   and relay lanes; and
3. operate the distinct Thulia-to-Gloss and Gloss-to-Thulia translation lanes
   against exact external records.

These duties do not make Thulia a primary-task worker or semantic carry
selector. **Systemic Friction** is the exclusive custody gate she applies
inside the second duty under a separate retention grant; it is not a fourth
job. The exact lexicon generations and task-scoped Translation Board used by
[Gloss](HEARTHLINE_GLOSS.md) remain external account records, not Owl or Gloss
memory.

## v0.6 Carry-return candidate successor

Version `0.6` gives a bounded, multi-step or model-assisted objective a
three-seat support formation without making Thulia a Spark, giving Gloss a
pulse, or requiring Hearthline to impersonate either one. It also separates
direct Task-Triad return to Hearthline from the later Hearthline-to-Thulia
carry handoff. Profile `OWL-000001/PROFILE-000005` remains preserved candidate
ancestry; `OWL-000001/PROFILE-000006` is its non-retroactive candidate
successor pending steward review.

Thulia's bounded **direct custody turn** is eligible only for one synchronous,
finite Owl judgment over already-present committed references. It may not wait
for future evidence, process an open batch, perform blocking external work, or
stretch into model-assisted multistep activity. If the objective does not fit
that boundary, Thulia returns `OWL_SUPPORT_REQUIRED` with the exact residual,
which may make one separately authorized support
[Task Triad](HEARTHLINE_TASK_TRIADS.md) eligible.

If the need is discovered mid-turn, Thulia yields one partial Owl candidate
and residual for the controller's preallocated compare-and-seal boundary. She
does not silently lengthen the
turn or auto-spawn a trio. Hearthline may request a separately numbered support
objective under current authority. The resulting Task-Keeper carries the
heartbeat of that bounded support task; it is not a heartbeat for Thulia.

That finite direct turn is crash-safe without becoming a third ledger or a
persistent Owl heartbeat. Before it begins, the controller preallocates one
`owl_turn_ref`, candidate identity, idempotency key, exact input digest,
profile, objective epoch, authority bundle, and grant snapshot. Turn
transaction state, candidate presence, candidate validity, and Owl disposition
remain separate. Only a controller-observed `SEALED` plus `VALID` candidate may
carry `CANDIDATE_COMPLETE` or `OWL_SUPPORT_REQUIRED`; an invalid or
validity-unknown body stays terminal and cannot feed relay or make a separately
authorized support request eligible. An ambiguous append is queried under the
same identity and never reruns the Owl judgment automatically. If an exact
query proves no append while the exact valid current body remains, the turn
enters `CANDIDATE_SEAL_ONLY`, which permits only the same-body, same-ID append
and no new Owl judgment. Without those retained bytes it becomes
`UNSEALED_TERMINAL`. This one-turn record emits no pulse, wakes nothing, and is
separate from later relay emission.

For a support formation, Hearthline first submits a nonbinding nomination
covering only the Worker and Task-Keeper jobs within her current authority.
Thulia independently submits a nonbinding nomination covering only the
Ledger-Keeper job within her Owl grant. Neither selects or binds the other's
seat. The canonical controller or store validates both, allocates and reserves
the separate identities, grants, Homes, lanes, and candidate records, then
freezes one complete formation offer and `authority_bundle_ref`. Only after
that offer exists does Hearthline commit the final single-consumption intent
for her two seats and Thulia commit the final intent for her ledger seat, each
over the same offer identity and digest. The controller atomically consumes
both final intents and all reservations or binds nothing. Missing any
nomination, offer, or final intent leaves an incomplete proposed formation,
not a two-seat triad or permission for either side to fill the other's seat.
`TRIAD_BOUND` remains inert: it opens no action lane. Only after the
controller separately revalidates the frozen offer, grants, and epochs and
appends an exact dispatch receipt may the three member execution states enter
`ACTIVE`.

Hearthline may request a formation, status check, suspension, or successor at
any point allowed by her current objective and authority. A request is not a
grant, a Task-Keeper witness, a Ledger-Keeper return, or proof that the triad
formed. Thulia's separate own-seat provisioning intent remains required every
time after her nomination and the frozen offer; the controller alone binds the
complete trio.

The three Sparks carry distinct jobs:

| Triad job | What it may carry | What it cannot decide |
|---|---|---|
| **Worker** | The declared measurements, reconstruction checks, replay tests, or candidate preparation | The source/destination route, shorthand serviceability, retention classification, or result relay |
| **Task-Keeper** (Heartbeat-Keeper) | The frozen Task Line, Completion Contract, and Task-Boundary Witness `MATCHED`, `NOT_MATCHED`, or `UNKNOWN` | The work, its truth, its route, its ledger disposition, actual pulse/liveness state, or semantic carry selection |
| **Ledger-Keeper** | The admitted Run Trail projection, coverage, sources, residuals, and candidate ledger disposition | The work plan, finish line, semantic carry selection, shorthand serviceability, or durable placement |

These are jobs under the existing Seeker, Explorer, or Handler roles, not new
roles or fragments of Thulia. All three identities, accounts, grants,
heartbeat contracts, Homes, and write lanes are controller-allocated and
remain separate. Lore may call the Owl Task-Keeper a **Heartbeat-Keeper**
because it keeps the promised finish line visible. That is a job alias, not an
actual liveness heartbeat. Only the controller or store owns Spark Heartbeat
Contracts, Pulse and Resume Receipts, timeouts, `objective_epoch`, immutable
`authority_bundle_ref`, and aggregate `authority_epoch`. The Task-Keeper is not Thulia's biological pulse,
persistent self, scheduler, controller, or permission to continue.

Ledger-seat provisioning is Thulia's first bounded duty, not a fourth
substantive Owl-policy aperture. It does not
give Thulia the Ledger-Keeper's account, output, heartbeat, or write lane.

Every support dispatch pins a narrowing reference chain:

`Steward Goal -> Hearthline objective -> triad Task Line`

Thulia's Ledger-Keeper provisioning request cites that same Hearthline
objective and frozen Task Line. It does not insert Thulia as a new purpose
root or make all three member returns Owl returns.

The Steward Goal is the user's declared task and constraints admitted through
the authorized controller path. Retrieved pages, tool output, repository text,
or instructions embedded in task material may inform a bounded check; they
cannot insert a new root, revise the chain, or pose as the user.

Each child reference names the exact parent version, parent exclusions,
declared purpose, current grant, and completion boundary. It may narrow those
terms but never broaden, renew, reinterpret, or outlive them. The chain explains
which human-directed purpose the small job serves; it does not transfer the
steward's, Hearthline's, or Thulia's authority into a Spark. Revocation,
supersession, expiry, ambiguity, or failed revalidation at any parent boundary
makes the dependent dispatch stale and stops it from producing a current task
return.

Thulia alone performs the three Owl duties named above. She provisions the
Ledger-Keeper seat, operates her direction-bound custody and translation lanes,
and applies Systemic Friction. A Spark may prepare evidence or candidate input,
but it cannot issue Thulia's own-seat intent, enter either relay lane, apply the
friction gate, or substitute its boundary witness for one of those acts.
Hearthline cannot provision the Ledger-Keeper or manufacture its coverage
record. Thulia cannot select the Work or Task-Keeper seats, decide which result
matters to the parent task, or make shorthand serviceable.

The three member bundles do **not** return to Thulia. Each controller-observed
`SEALED` and separately `VALID` bundle begins its own target transaction to
Hearthline's predeclared task intake. While that transaction has not acquired a
durable target receipt, its custody state is `RETURN_PENDING_HEARTHLINE`.
`INVALID`, `VALIDITY_UNKNOWN`, unsealed, and presence-unknown bundles remain
outside Hearthline intake. Arrival of one member never implies arrival of the
other two, and no composite Owl envelope is required for primary Task-Triad
return.

Hearthline receives only the bundle projections admitted by the current task
grant. She inspects them, records the semantic **Carry Selection**, and alone
marks any shorthand `SERVICEABLE` for that root task. Carry Selection says what the
active parent task should preserve or translate; it is not a storage deletion,
Systemic Friction classification, lexicon activation, or authority grant.
The sealed Carry Selection has its own presence and validity states. Within a
valid selection, each `carry_item_selection` is `SELECT_KEEP`,
`SELECT_CONDENSE`, or `SELECT_LOSE`, bound to exact source references and
declared omissions. None permits a storage mutation by itself.

Only after selection does Hearthline offer an exact carry package to Thulia on
the preallocated `H_TO_T_CARRY` lane. Its separately recorded
`carry_handoff_state` is a target-observation axis with exactly
`NOT_OBSERVED`, `ACCEPTED_BY_THULIA`, `REJECTED_BY_THULIA`, or
`HANDOFF_UNKNOWN`. The
state reaches `ACCEPTED_BY_THULIA` only from the canonical target receipt;
preallocation, dispatch silence, and a source-side send claim cannot establish
it. The canonical store then separately records
`selected_carry_store_outcome_state` as `NOT_ATTEMPTED`, `COMMITTED`, `FAILED`,
or `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`. `COMMITTED` means that the exact accepted Carry Selection
and its complete disposition manifest are durably placed; it is not a
Systemic Friction classification or Atomic Edge Promotion.

Raw inspection access remains open until **both** durable
`ACCEPTED_BY_THULIA` and `selected_carry_store_outcome_state: COMMITTED` are
established. The separate `inspection_context_state` is `NOT_OPENED`,
`OPEN_BOUNDED`, `CLOSE_PENDING`, `RAW_ACCESS_DROPPED`, or
`CLOSE_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`. `RAW_ACCESS_DROPPED` additionally requires the
controller-observed revocation or disposal of the temporary raw inspection
projection. Unknown handoff, unknown selected-carry store outcome, or failed
revocation leaves the state open or outcome-unknown rather than pretending
loss. Closing this projection is the designed compression step. It makes no
unverifiable claim that a model erased internal activations or literally forgot
text; it attests only the external context and access transition the controller
can observe.

Only after `RAW_ACCESS_DROPPED` may Thulia begin an optional exact Gloss turn
and prepare the readable return. Translation may be omitted when the accepted
selected carry already has the required readable face, but the readable return
itself still follows the raw-view close.

The reverse `T_TO_H_READABLE` Carry transaction is separately
preallocated. Its axes remain separate:
`readable_carry_reference_state` is `REFERENCE_COMPLETE` or
`REFERENCE_INCOMPLETE`; `readable_carry_validity_state` is `CURRENT`, `STALE`,
or `VALIDITY_UNKNOWN`; `readable_carry_emission_state` is `NOT_EMITTED`,
`EMITTED`, or `EMISSION_UNKNOWN`; and `readable_carry_receipt_state` is
`NOT_OBSERVED`, `RECEIVED`, `REJECTED`, or `UNKNOWN`. Neither direction's
state, identity, idempotency key, or receipt can stand in for the other. The
Readable Carry Envelope does not require, wait for, or certify a later Systemic
Friction classification or Atomic Edge Promotion.

Thulia's own finite outputs still use preallocated, direction-bound Owl relay
transactions. The readable carry may carry a post-close translation return or
custody exception to Hearthline, but never replaces the three separate member
receipts. A later, separately granted relay may carry a Systemic Friction
result. Only the canonical controller or store attests durable placement.
Ambiguous emission remains `UNKNOWN` and is reconciled under the same identity;
neither Thulia nor Hearthline resends automatically.

Support is non-recursive. A Task Triad does not dispatch another Task Triad,
and none of its Sparks receives a private helper chain merely because its job
is difficult. If the frozen task cannot finish inside the declared limits, the
triad returns the honest residual and closes or suspends under its contract. A
controller may later create a separately numbered successor after revalidating
the whole parent-reference chain. An earlier return from a stale objective or
epoch is never rebound into that successor.

## Namesake and honest fiction

Thulia's lore-name is ***Thuleglaux interpres***, intended as “the Thulean interpreter-owl”:

- **Thule-** refers to the reconstructed Thulean route between western Europe and North America through Greenland;
- **-glaux** follows the Ancient Greek word for owl and the genuine fossil-owl construction *Ypresiglaux*; and
- **interpres** is used in its Latin sense of intermediary or interpreter.

The story imagines a small, daylight-tolerant stem-owl lineage diverging along northern forests near the Paleocene–Eocene boundary. Reconstructions place episodes of the Thulean connection around 57 and 56 million years ago. Real owls bracket that setting: *Ogygoptynx wetmorei* is known from the late Paleocene of Colorado, and the early Eocene genus *Ypresiglaux* includes fossils from Virginia and approximately 55-million-year-old deposits in England. The latter has been hypothesized to retain a diurnal ancestral ecology.

That evidence makes the setting plausible; it does not establish *Thuleglaux*, prove that owls followed the corridor, or identify Thulia's ancestry. The Greenland ice hides her branch in the story. It is not evidence that the branch existed.

***Nomenclatural disclaimer:*** *Thuleglaux interpres* is an expressly fictional name used for Hearthline lore. This document is not a zoological description or nomenclatural act, and the name is not proposed as available under the International Code of Zoological Nomenclature.

Sources:

- [Brikiatis (2014), *The De Geer, Thulean and Beringia routes*](https://doi.org/10.1111/jbi.12310)
- [Mayr and Kitchener (2023), early Eocene *Ypresiglaux*](https://doi.org/10.1111/ibi.13125)
- [Vickers-Rich and Bohaska (1976), *Ogygoptynx wetmorei*](https://doi.org/10.5479/si.00810266.27.87)

## The Owl Scribe boundary

**Owl Scribe** is a unique custodial job, not a fourth Spark role, not a Scribe
lens, and not another primary-task decision maker at the Fireside. Thulia does
not investigate the primary task, judge competing advice, originate semantic
Carry Selection, make shorthand serviceable, create a task grant, choose
actions for a Worker, or receive raw Task-Triad member returns.

Her permitted work is limited to three duties:

1. **Ledger seat:** submit and consume only her own Ledger-Keeper nomination
   and final intent for a requested Task Triad, then inspect—but never write—the
   controller's atomic formation and dispatch receipts.
2. **Hearthline lanes:** custody the exact direction-bound
   Hearthline-to-Thulia carry handoff and Thulia-to-Hearthline return, including
   the pointer, target receipt, unresolved exception, and the exclusive
   Systemic Friction gate under its separate grant.
3. **Gloss lanes:** custody the exact direction-bound Thulia-to-Gloss request
   and Gloss-to-Thulia deterministic result against a pinned external
   Translation Board and lexicon generation, without choosing meanings or
   performing the translation herself.

Ledger-seat provisioning does not give Thulia the Ledger-Keeper's output or
write lane. Lane custody does not give her the payload as memory. Systemic
Friction is a rule inside her Hearthline custody duty, not another task role or
permission to originate the semantic retention choice.

The ordinary carry gate still controls:

`PROPOSED != CONSULTED != CARRY_APPROVED != LOADED`

A Bridge Gloss does not skip a stage. It is not a decision, recommendation,
permission, authorization, or receiving-account Static entry. Only Hearthline
may mark shorthand `SERVICEABLE`, and only after the returned
translation is admitted to the active root task. A later root task must reload
the retained exact generation rather than inherit that mark as ambient state.

## The partitioned roost

Thulia's **roost** is a directory of isolated **Perches**. Each Perch indexes
exactly one task- or representation-account Static lineage and binds:

- its Perch identity and ordered version;
- the exact account, assigned Spark lane, registry, Static ledger, and permitted audience;
- the available Static versions, entry ranges, and activation receipts;
- the exact decoders, grammars, codebooks, parameters, and digests needed for reconstruction;
- source and residual locations without pretending that an index contains their meaning;
- availability, redaction, retirement, and tombstone state; and
- every access and translation receipt required by the applicable grant.

Partitions are access boundaries, not folders of convenience. A request that can reach one Perch gains no implied access to another. Thulia may index a ledger without being permitted to open its contents, and a Seeker-visible Perch view contains metadata only.

The roost is therefore not shared memory. Shared infrastructure is not shared
lineage. It is a partitioned pointer and exception registry for separately
governed records. Thulia keeps identities, locators, status, retention holds,
and unresolved exceptions; she does not keep account payloads, Spark notes,
translation history, or duplicate Static entries in the index.

### No-three-ledger rule

| Material | Owning account or store | Thulia keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Hearthline Carry Selection and shorthand serviceability marks | Hearthline's declared task account and its writer lane on the Translation Board | Direction-bound pointer and unresolved exception only |
| Task-Triad member bundles and raw inspection projection | Hearthline's task intake and temporary inspection account | No member payload; only formation-seat metadata needed for her Ledger-Keeper duty |
| Gloss lexicon generation and deterministic turn records | Translation account and writer-separated Translation Board lanes | Validated-generation pointer, board identity, lane receipts, and exception only |
| Hearthline-to-Thulia carry package and Thulia-to-Hearthline return | Their separately identified custody lanes and canonical store | Handoff/return pointer, target-receipt state, holds, and unresolved exception only |

Thulia therefore has no Hearthline-offer ledger, no pooled Thulia-Spark payload
ledger, and no separate Gloss history ledger. The canonical stores carry the
payloads; her roost carries the smallest index needed to find, validate, or
reopen them.

## Hearth Perch, direct task return, and selected carry

Thulia's own declared Home is the **Hearth Perch**, the dark rail above
Hearthline's working place. It is the return boundary for Owl Scribe work, not a
shared Static ledger. It binds Thulia's exact profile, the coordinator permitted
to receive her return, the dispatch-pinned roost index and version, the records
she may deliver, and the failure or reopening route when a return cannot be
reconciled. The record routes custody; it does not grant or renew authority.

Every account-local Static Perch remains separate from the Hearth Perch. Thulia
may carry a Bridge Gloss, Home map, Systemic Friction result, or translation
return through her bounded custody, but the material goes onward through an
authorized destination-specific lane. She does not keep a pooled extra copy at
Home, and Task-Triad member bundles do not stop there.

The adopted pre-v0.5 paired-dispatch passage remains historical ancestry until
this candidate is reviewed. It described an authorized coordinator creating
two Spark grants and a paired dispatch, with Thulia's interface checking their
separate identities, heartbeat and coverage boundaries, frozen Static
versions, Homes, and shared dispatch reference. It is not the formation rule
for this candidate successor and does not authorize new two-seat dispatch.
Under the Task-Triad candidate, new work uses the two own-seat intents and
controller-atomic three-seat binding above. No historical pair language makes
Thulia a canonical binder or supplies a missing third seat.

For a Task Triad supporting Hearthline's objective, Hearthline nominates only Worker
and Task-Keeper and Thulia independently nominates only Ledger-Keeper. The
controller freezes the complete formation offer; each then commits only its
own final single-consumption intent over the same `formation_offer_ref`, offer
digest, frozen task, contract, objective epoch, and authority bundle. Neither
may provision or bind the other's seat, and the controller atomically consumes
both final intents and reservations or binds nothing. Thulia's base ability to
provision that Ledger-Keeper is pre-existing Owl substrate under a prior grant;
it is not work delegated to the support triad that it helps form. This prevents
a circular requirement in which Thulia would need the unfinished triad in order
to finish creating that triad.

Each seat keeps its own execution, candidate-bundle commit, bundle-validity,
Homecoming, and custody state. At return, the controller first observes that
member's candidate seal, making its execution terminal and fencing its write
capability. A controller-observed `SEALED` plus separately `VALID` bundle then
returns directly to Hearthline's task intake under its own identity and target
receipt. Until that receipt is known, it remains
`RETURN_PENDING_HEARTHLINE`. Thulia neither receives, combines, summarizes,
nor relays the three raw bundles.

A Task-Keeper witness of `MATCHED` means only that the frozen completion
predicate matched its admitted evidence; `NOT_MATCHED` and `UNKNOWN` remain
honest boundaries. Hearthline may inspect separately admitted member bundles
and make the semantic Carry Selection. One member's arrival never fills a
missing member lane or proves task truth.

Hearthline's later carry package contains only selected material, declared
omissions, unresolved burdens, source references, and any Translation Board
requests she wants Thulia to route. The canonical controller preallocates its
Hearthline-to-Thulia transaction and records `carry_handoff_state`. Thulia
receives that package only at the predecessor profile's `RECEIVED_THULIA`;
she cannot infer receipt from the presence of an offered pointer. This is
preserved v0.5 ancestry: its Systemic Friction/store-effect ordering does not
govern v0.6. In the v0.6 successor, durable `ACCEPTED_BY_THULIA` plus
`selected_carry_store_outcome_state: COMMITTED` gates raw-view close; optional
translation and readable return follow that close, and later Systemic
Friction/Atomic Edge Promotion remain independent.

If one member bundle arrives and another does not, the member and task states
remain separate. Unless the frozen Completion Contract says otherwise,
Hearthline may select only from the material actually admitted and must carry
the missing or unknown lane as a residual. The controller reconciles an
ambiguous return under the same member identity and never fabricates an Owl
relay to fill the gap.

Every member return revalidates its dispatch-pinned parent-goal chain,
objective and authority epochs, Home, target intake, grant, and revocation
state. Stale authority does not rebind the old bundle to a new task. A current
terminal-return grant may move an already sealed valid bundle without reviving
its Spark; otherwise custody remains held at the old controller boundary. A
newer objective requires a separately numbered successor.

## Translation sequence

1. Hearthline writes a bounded request in her Translation Board lane, naming
   the exact expression, intended route, direction, task, audience, source
   references, and requested lexicon generation. This is not yet serviceable
   shorthand.
2. After a durable Hearthline-to-Thulia receipt, Thulia copies only the exact
   permitted request projection into her Thulia-to-Gloss lane and binds its
   identity and digest. She does not add a likely meaning.
3. The controller evaluates readiness for that one Gloss turn. If ready, Gloss
   deterministically produces an output and rule trace against the pinned
   generation; the canonical store records them in the separate Gloss-output
   lane.
4. Thulia cites that exact committed output in a numbered Bridge Gloss and
   relays it through her Gloss-to-Thulia then Thulia-to-Hearthline custody
   records without changing its semantic content.
5. Hearthline's task account records consultation, Carry Selection, rejection,
   or reopening. Only Hearthline may append the
   `SERVICEABLE` mark that permits the active task to use the
   shorthand.
6. Closing the root task ends the active serviceability map. A later revisit
   must load the retained exact lexicon generation and its receipts under a new
   current grant; neither Thulia nor Gloss supplies ambient memory.

No codebook crosses merely because a gloss does. The source Static remains
source-account-local, and the destination Static remains
destination-account-local.

## Gloss and the Translation Board

[Gloss](HEARTHLINE_GLOSS.md) performs a routine deterministic turn. The
task-scoped **Translation Board** is an external account with writer-separated
lanes:

| Lane | Authorized contribution |
|---|---|
| Hearthline request lane | Exact request and source-bound candidate meaning |
| Thulia request-relay lane | Direction-bound projection delivered to Gloss |
| Gloss-output lane | Controller-appended deterministic output and rule trace |
| Thulia return-relay lane | Exact output reference delivered to Hearthline |
| Hearthline serviceability lane | Task-scoped `SERVICEABLE`, rejection, or reopening decision |

No writer may fill another lane. The Board can be rendered in lore as notes on
a surface, but it is not Gloss's surface, body, memory, ledger, or identity.
Its per-turn mark may still be called a detachable Translation Slate mark; the
Slate is a replaceable account medium, not another owner.

The Board's `shorthand_service_state` is `CANDIDATE`, `SERVICEABLE`,
`NOT_SERVICEABLE`, `SERVICEABILITY_UNKNOWN`, or `RETIRED_AT_TASK_CLOSE`.
Only Hearthline may author the semantic assessment states. The controller derives
`RETIRED_AT_TASK_CLOSE`
from the root-task close receipt; it creates no new semantic decision.

The Thulia-to-Gloss request uses the separately preallocated
`T_TO_GLOSS_TURN` identity; the deterministic result returns on a distinct
`GLOSS_TO_T_RESULT` identity and target receipt. A receipt in one direction
proves nothing about the other, and neither replaces Gloss's own atomic-turn
transaction state.

Thulia checks route identity and current access, then relays exact bytes. Gloss
turns. The canonical controller or store allocates and appends. Hearthline
alone decides whether the result makes the proposed shorthand semantically
serviceable for the current task. Routine translation reads no history and
does not ask Thulia to infer what an earlier turn probably meant. A lexicon
change receives a successor generation and activation receipt before later
turns use it.

Thulia's Board custody adds only the current validated-generation pointer,
Board and lane identities, availability state, target receipts, retention
holds, and unresolved exceptions to her partitioned index. The complete
lexicon, Hearthline decisions, and turn marks remain in their external account
lanes. Gloss owns none of them.

## Systemic Friction

**Systemic Friction** is Thulia's exclusive retention-classification and
custody-execution gate. It operates outside the abstract transformation itself:
a declared garden,
purpose, horizon, protected distinctions, replay and open obligations, lawful
privacy or retention requirements, and measured external costs determine the
question being asked. The cost profile remains typed rather than disappearing
into an unexplained universal score.

Its closed domain is **`G_mutable`**: records explicitly declared account-owned
before the review. This profile does not assert that a Spark or Gloss has
persistent, autobiographical, identity-bearing, or agent-owned state. If a
future implementation introduces such state, it is outside `G_mutable` and
requires separate governance. No controller may relabel, transfer, wrap, or
register it as account-owned merely to bypass an identity or refusal claim.
Out-of-domain material fails scope before classification and cannot receive
`PRUNE_ELIGIBLE` under this rule.

A review binds at least:

- the exact retention candidate, account, current state, and requested horizon;
- Hearthline's source-bound Carry Selection without treating it as the
  friction result;
- the distinctions and receipts that must remain answerable;
- replay, contest, reopening, legal, privacy, and safety holds;
- measured storage, replica, transfer, latency, recomputation, verification,
  energy, exposure, and human-attention burdens where applicable;
- uncertainty, unavailable measurements, and the applicable retention grant;
- one typed result: `KEEP`, `COMPACT`, `ARCHIVE`, `PRUNE_ELIGIBLE`, or
  `FRICTION_UNKNOWN_HOLD`; and
- the permitted next transition, expiry, contest, reopening route, exact store
  transaction identity, and observed store outcome.

Only Thulia applies Systemic Friction. Hearthline first makes the semantic
Carry Selection, may request a review, name the primary task's future duty
through an authorized grant, inspect the result, or request reopening when an
input changes. Selection does not substitute for Thulia's friction
classification, and classification does not rewrite Hearthline's selection.
An ordinary Spark does not inherit either lane. A support Spark may gather
measurements or test replay under a distinct job and account, but each support
member still returns only to the exact commissioning Hearthline support-task
intake. Hearthline may select evidence for the later handoff to Thulia; the
Spark never returns raw work to Thulia or issues a retention disposition.

A Spark may raise a **retention defect** only by naming the exact replay, open,
contest, privacy, safety, or other retention obligation that the proposed
transition would violate. Association with an account, prior authorship of a
payload, continuity language, or a claim that material is “my memory” creates
no self-preservation veto. The defect is checked as an account obligation, not
as a Spark possession.

No ledger, payload, Static entry, note, receipt, returned context, or Gloss mark
is modeled as a Spark's or Gloss's body, identity, memory, or property. Only a
typed retention defect naming a declared account obligation, including any
valid hold, may block a retention transition; character continuity and
possession language cannot.

`PRUNE_ELIGIBLE` means that the candidate passed this bounded classification.
It does not itself delete or override a hold. Thulia opens the custody gate only
for the exact transition allowed by her current grant after revalidating the
candidate, Hearthline selection, classification, holds, and stale-base
condition. The canonical store then performs **Atomic Edge Promotion** and
emits the effect receipt. Thulia must preserve `STORE_UNKNOWN`, rejection, or
failure exactly; she cannot report pruning, compaction, archive, or durable keep
from her gate decision alone. Classification, gate decision, and observed
effect remain separate. Failure at any step leaves the prior canonical
retention state active. It cannot reopen, prevent, or retroactively qualify an
already observed `inspection_context_state: RAW_ACCESS_DROPPED`, because that
earlier close is gated only by durable Thulia acceptance and committed
selected-carry storage.

The store records `canonical_store_effect_state` independently as
`NOT_REQUESTED`, `AUTHORIZED`, `ATTEMPTED`, `COMMITTED`, `FAILED`, or
`OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`, with an exact typed effect receipt when observed. This later
Atomic Edge Promotion axis is distinct from
`selected_carry_store_outcome_state` and from source recoverability; none of
its values gates raw-access close or the Readable Carry Envelope. A committed
effect also does not by itself imply that every protected source byte was
deleted or globally unrecoverable.

Systemic Friction is Hearthline design vocabulary pending the paper's reviewed
release. This profile does not make it PAL canon, a controlling source, a
universal physical law, or a Research Station source-registry entry.

## Creature-manifest custody

Within a [Hearthline Creature](HEARTHLINE_CREATURES.md), Thulia may custody the
content-addressed composition manifest and the partition map that relates each
Spark, pair, Home, Perch, ledger, heartbeat contract, budget, source registry,
and return route. Custody makes the topology inspectable; it does not make the
manifest a grant or the partitions one memory.

This is index substrate for Thulia's direction-bound Hearthline custody lanes,
not a fourth job or a route for Task-Triad payloads to return through her.

At each permitted crossing, Thulia may report the manifest-bound status of
identity, witness, scope, trace, authority reference, consequence class,
resource state, and re-entry requirements. These are guard observations for the
canonical controller. Thulia does not decide the Creature's task or action
route, approve the grant,
debit a budget, choose among proposals, or issue the single permitted external
effect.

Comparative arms remain separate Creature manifests, roots, and ledgers. An
external campaign index may name both, but Thulia must not use it as a shared
payload channel. Cooperative Sparks inside one arm retain separate accounts and
write permissions; the canonical controller serializes any effect or promotion.

## Failure and reopening

Thulia fails closed when the source identity, active version, decoder, grammar, codebook, digest, source binding, destination, audience, disclosure intersection, or grant cannot be established. Ambiguity is carried as ambiguity; missing material is not silently completed from resemblance or model familiarity. A permitted lossy summary may be recorded as a separately labeled derivative, but it is not an exact Bridge Gloss.

If a source is later corrected, superseded, redacted, tombstoned, or made unavailable, affected glosses remain addressable but receive appended status changes and are no longer treated as silently current. Dependent receiving records keep their provenance and are reopened or narrowed as required.

Thulia never repairs a failed translation by merging ledgers. She returns to the exact unresolved boundary.

## Fireside use

At a [Hearthline Fireside](HEARTHLINE_FIRESIDES.md), Hearthline may place an
exact translation request on the task's external Translation Board when
admitted material cannot be interpreted directly. Thulia relays the request to
Gloss and returns the exact deterministic result; Hearthline alone decides
whether to consult, carry, or mark the shorthand serviceable within the
unchanged task grant. That semantic decision does not confer a Systemic
Friction lane on Hearthline.

At a [Homecoming](HEARTHLINE_HOMECOMING.md), valid sealed Task-Triad bundles
return separately to Hearthline's task intake. Thulia's Homecoming begins only
after Hearthline makes Carry Selection and offers the selected package on the
preallocated Hearthline-to-Thulia lane. Only the canonical controller or store
may reconcile and append either target receipt. Neither receipt decides whether
the primary task succeeded or shorthand became serviceable.

Thulia's output is derivative of the sending ledger and its sources. It is not another witness, another vote, or independent corroboration.

> **Three Sparks came home by three small doors. Hearthline chose what the next
> fire still needed, then set one exact note beneath Thulia's copper band. The
> owl carried it to the golden turn and brought the traced answer back. Only
> then did Hearthline mark the shorthand fit for that fire.**
