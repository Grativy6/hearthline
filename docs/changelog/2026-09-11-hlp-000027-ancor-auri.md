## What changed

Added [Ancor Auri](../../lore/ANCOR_AURI.md) as a canonical fictional
Hearthline character and linked him from the lore entrance. Ancor is a
leprechaun who places a provisional anchor near possible game-world gold,
asks what would have to be true just before reaching it, and offers Hearthline
a backward route to test from her side. The story shows an initial guess
revised after a game-world observation, with the game alone confirming `WIN`.
The public history and latest-change entrances record this lore successor.

## Why

Christopher named Ancor Auri from a playful shortening of *anchor* and the
Latin root for gold. He chose a leprechaun rather than a gremlin because the
role is a deliberate guide toward a possible prize, not an accidental fault
or a creature that breaks machinery. The name gives the backward-looking
concept a distinct fictional face without making him the whole game player.

## Preserved boundaries

Ancor is fictional and bounded to a declared game in this vignette. The gold
mark is a possible target, not certain foreknowledge or an evaluator result.
His proposed causal route remains revisable and unpromoted. Hearthline plans,
tests, and acts forward through the game's admitted controls and finite limits;
the game evaluator alone owns terminal `WIN` confirmation. Ancor cannot
command Hearthline, replace a controller, alter a gate by decree, access an
outside system, or enlarge a task or lease.

Ancor is distinct from the literal gremlins already present in
[Before the Rain](../../lore/BEFORE_THE_RAIN.md). CAP's fictional common desk
remains a separate institution with no sovereignty or task authority. This
addition changes neither its record nor earlier lore. Christopher D. Pang
remains author and steward; AI drafting is tool assistance, not co-authorship
or authority.
