## Why

The formal CHARTER manuscript gives the orchestration layer exact mathematical
names, but the original Creature language makes its relationships memorable to
future Hearthline. The two vocabularies serve different jobs. CHARTER controls
formal claims; the lore names help an operator see a bounded formation without
turning it into one larger will.

The companion also closes a counting seam. The attractive picture of three
roles repeated around one center can look like a `3n+1` population law. The
formal bridge is stronger and more careful: each Trio proposes an observable
that may split an unresolved task-fiber, the Creature is their finite joint
lift, and the Heart is a deterministic completion coordinate. The Heart adds
readability without adding a role instance or state distinction.

The story's release lesson records another practical boundary. Hearthline
should send the smallest honest Creature that can meet the current frozen
finish line. A passed blocker permits release; a remaining idea becomes a
versioned residual. Systemic Friction may include discovery time lost to
continued polishing, but no cost estimate may convert a failed blocker into
success.

## Preserved boundaries

- CHARTER v1.0 controls the mathematical terminology and claim ceiling. The
  Creature words are lore and design aliases; they do not transfer a theorem,
  certificate, authority, implementation, or physical interpretation.
- The inherited v0.1–v0.2 Lean core remains delimited. The TTC/BPS,
  Systemic-Friction, translation, selected-carry, release, and
  minimum-asymmetry layers remain manuscript-level proposals without a
  dedicated post-core Lean receipt, implementation, calibration, deployment,
  or independent review.
- A Prime Seed or Circuit Garden receipt records acceptance under a declared
  checker and aperture. It does not manufacture truth, prove checker
  completeness, grant authority, or transfer a circuit theorem into number
  theory.
- The Task-Keeper's lore heartbeat carries a frozen finish line. It is not a
  scheduler, keepalive, runtime heartbeat, progress claim, or liveness witness.
  Consequential liveness requires a separately sourced, timed, uncertain, and
  receipted observation.
- Every added Light Trio must either split an unresolved task-fiber or carry a
  separately declared operational or evidentiary purpose. More Sparks do not
  automatically add information, intelligence, diversity, reliability, or
  performance.
- Hearthline nominates only Worker and Task-Keeper seats and owns semantic
  Carry Selection and task-scoped shorthand serviceability. Thulia nominates
  only Ledger-Keeper seats, routes selected carry, and alone classifies
  Systemic Friction under a separate grant. Gloss remains a pinned,
  task-memoryless deterministic transducer. None absorbs another's job.
- `SELECT_LOSE` is a semantic selection, not deletion. `PRUNE_ELIGIBLE` is a
  classification, not an effect grant. Atomic Edge Promotion is a separately
  authorized controller or canonical-writer transition, and it does not prove
  physical or global erasure.
- The lore's forgetting scene means observed closure of an active task's raw
  inspection handles after durable selected-carry acceptance. It does not
  establish provider forgetting, backup deletion, canonical-source deletion,
  or unrecoverability from every other route.
- The optional minimum-asymmetry frame applies only when declared. Even-sized
  Creatures remain valid; the larger bank gains no truth or authority; its
  Eisenstein index may be composite; and every primality claim needs an
  independent witness.
- No fictional character is identified with an OpenAI model, deployment, or
  persistent model self. Creature, Hearthline, Thulia, Gloss, Sparks, Heart,
  and garden language makes no claim about consciousness, personhood,
  experience, memory possession, standing, consent, or authority.
- Christopher D. Pang remains sole author, branch founder, steward, and
  publication authority. AI systems assist as tools and are not authors,
  co-authors, witnesses, adopters, stewards, or mathematical authorities.
