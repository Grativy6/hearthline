## Why

HLP-000012 was prepared before the Zenodo Publish action and correctly closed
with the DOI marked reserved. It explicitly left publication observation and
exact source registration open for a later append-only successor. The public
record now exists, so leaving current-facing surfaces at `deposit pending`
would make the live source map and agent guidance stale.

The successor records only the newly observed publication boundary. A public
DOI and matching inventory make the artifact citable and identify the released
bytes. They do not make the manuscript peer reviewed, prove its claims, run its
post-core architecture, establish an ARC effect, or authorize any action.
