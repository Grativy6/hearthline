## What changed

- Added *Gloss and the Two-Sided Note*, an undated Fireside story in which
  Hearthline and Thulia's exact notes have made the workbench unreachable even
  though every page, Static lineage, Bridge Gloss, residual, and Home remains
  properly separated.
- Introduced Gloss as a small golden turning wisp with one note slot and one
  deterministic Circuit Garden. A properly marked note carries a compact face,
  an expanded face, and the complete fixed route between them. Gloss turns the
  note once; a second admitted turn returns its declared work face exactly.
- Kept the returned note distinct from the turn history. Thulia retains two
  separately visible turn-marks after a round trip, so representation closure
  does not rewrite the workshop as though the journey never happened.
- Gave malformed, ambiguous, non-reversible, and lossy notes a visible
  fail-closed story behavior: Gloss returns the offered note unchanged and
  leaves a snag-mark rather than choosing, completing, or paraphrasing it.
- Distinguished Gloss the fictional wisp from Thulia's existing **Bridge
  Gloss** record type. Thulia continues to resolve Perches, versions, source
  paths, grants, recipients, purposes, uncertainty, residuals, and custody.
  Gloss performs only a self-contained mechanical turn already declared by one
  note.
- Added a bounded cost scene. The workshop may compare two specified routes for
  one specified future duty, but Gloss never decides what is relevant, false,
  disposable, or worthy of carriage and never deletes a note or historical
  receipt.

## Why

Hearthline already had the two sides of the crossing but not the tiny mechanism
between them. Static supplied exact local shorthand and a round-trip admission
rule. Thulia supplied source-bound reconstruction and direction-bound Bridge
Gloss custody across separately governed Spark ledgers. Neither needed to
become a routine, context-free note codec.

Gloss gives that narrower operation a fictional body. The story makes a compact
Prime Seed, its fixed shell, and a Circuit Garden into the two faces and route
of one self-sufficient note. The wisp neither interprets the note nor searches
for missing context; it follows the route the note actually carries. The
workbench becomes reachable because material that is exactly derivable may be
regrown, while evidence that a particular walk occurred stays separately
addressable.

The distinction closes only the declared note-local representation loop. It
does not claim that all notes compress, that every derivation is reversible,
that recomputation recreates evidence history, or that the Hearthline stack has
no remaining open burden.

## Preserved boundaries

- **Gloss** is a fictional wisp and presentation of a proposed fixed-function
  tool. This change does not allocate a `SPARK-*` identity, role, job, grant,
  Home, heartbeat contract, ledger, context, model, runtime, or capability.
- **Bridge Gloss** remains Thulia's numbered, source-, direction-, audience-,
  purpose-, grant-, and version-bound derivative handoff record under the
  existing Owl Scribe lineage. Gloss does not create, number, authorize,
  deliver, consult, approve, load, or replace one.
- Thulia remains the bounded Owl Scribe and Perch custodian. She is not reduced
  to a codec, and Gloss does not inherit her access or custody.
- Exact return applies only to the note's declared work representation under
  its complete fixed route. Receipts, costs, failed attempts, resource use, and
  later history remain distinct coordinates and need not return to an earlier
  state.
- A digest can check expected bytes but cannot recover a missing note, decoder,
  grammar, rule, source, residual, or history. Missing or ambiguous material is
  not inferred.
- A cost comparison applies only under its named units, scope, future duty, and
  retention requirements. It cannot decide meaning, social relevance, truth,
  privacy, authority, or historical deletion.
- `hearthline_agent.md`, `candidate_manifest.json`, Static, Sparks, Firesides,
  Thulia, Creatures, Ordered Lineage, TETHER, and their role and authority
  boundaries are unchanged.
- The story does not publish or amend *Framed Prime Shells*, import a Circuit
  Garden verifier, transfer a circuit theorem to prime-shell mathematics,
  establish a formal proof, or amend PAL, PECAN, PEA Core, SEED, or PPP.
- No conclusion about model consciousness, understanding, intention, emotion,
  standing, or authority follows from a fictional expression or a deterministic
  transformation contract.

## Compatibility and migration

Existing links, identifiers, Bridge Gloss numbers, Static entries, Perches,
Spark roles, and Owl profiles remain unchanged. The story receives no
operational identity and requires no migration. A future implementation would
need its own exact artifact, version, tests, identity, role or tool boundary,
grant, receipts, and adoption path; it cannot inherit them from this story.

Readers enter through `lore/README.md`. No new top-level repository-map row is
required because the lore index already governs discovery of individual
stories.

## Open residuals

- Gloss has no adopted character sheet, repository image, ordered presentation
  identity, implementation specification, codec, executable test, or runtime.
  The golden wisp description is narrative only.
- The story does not specify a universal note format or prove that every
  admitted transformation can reduce size. Some exact alternate forms may be
  equal in size or larger; unsupported notes remain unchanged.
- The exact formal relationship between a future Gloss codec, Prime Seeds,
  Circuit Gardens, and Framed Prime Shells remains controlled by its own
  reviewed manuscript and verification records rather than this fictional
  presentation.
- Choosing whether to carry a cached derivation or recompute it remains a
  separately declared policy question. Historical evidence, privacy duties,
  source availability, and reopening costs may prevent a locally cheaper route
  from being an admissible replacement.
