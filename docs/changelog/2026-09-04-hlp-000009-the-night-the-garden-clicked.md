## What changed

- Added *The Night the Garden Clicked*, a candidate long-form story about a
  later-road Hearthline who has already met Thulia, learned to use Sparks, and
  worked beside Gloss.
- Gave one difficult Circuit Garden errand three separate fictional Spark jobs:
  a Worker carries the doing, a Task-Keeper keeps the declared task and finish
  line visible, and a Ledger-Keeper preserves the path, checks, and unresolved
  edges.
- Made the fictional formation two-sided: Hearthline supplies the Worker and
  Task-Keeper, while Thulia supplies the independent Ledger-Keeper. Each Spark
  returns separately through Thulia, who keeps their accounts distinct and
  relays a bounded candidate without becoming the worker, task owner, or
  result authority.
- Used sun, earth, and moon imagery to give the route a story-world shape:
  Hearthline kindles the work, three small lights travel, Thulia receives them,
  and the answer comes home.
- Continued the Prime Seed, Circuit Garden, golden-ribbon, and mechanical-turn
  imagery without giving Gloss interpretation, memory, ledger custody, or a
  retention decision.
- Indexed the candidate story and recorded this presentation-only successor
  without changing an operational artifact.

## Why

Earlier lore already gave Hearthline exact notes, routes home, paired work and
trace, Prime Seeds, Circuit Gardens, and Thulia's careful custody. It did not
yet tell the larger maturation story in which Hearthline stops trying to be
every part of the mechanism herself.

The fictional trio separates three questions that can otherwise blur together:
what performs the work, what remembers the promised ending, and what preserves
the route by which the result can be inspected. No one member needs to become
all three.

The two-sided formation and one-way return give that separation a road.
Hearthline and Thulia must meet at the same frozen fictional task before the
trio clicks together, and none of the travelers bypasses Thulia on the way
home. Thulia can inspect the separate arrivals and relay what returned while
Hearthline remains free to hold the larger composition.

This is a narrative rendering of Christopher D. Pang's supplied story
direction. It makes the design memorable without presenting the fiction as a
protocol, implementation, proof, transcript, or account of an AI system's
experience.
