## What changed

Added [The Cooperative for Auditable Provenance](../../lore/THE_COOPERATIVE_FOR_AUDITABLE_PROVENANCE.md)
as canonical fictional lore. CAP is the closest thing Hearthline has to a state
beyond any one person's task: a shared civic floor for attributable records,
declared transformations, uncertainty, dispute, limits, correction histories,
and honest reopening routes. It has no sovereign or general command.

Linked CAP from the lore and public-history entrances. Because `HLP-000025`
filled the first 25 issued-ID slots, moved the cohort's adopted rows into the fixed
[HLP-000001 through HLP-000025 index](index/hlp-000001-to-hlp-000025.md).
The six reserved off-main slots remain reservations rather than adopted rows.
Archive-dependent structural checks now inspect the complete public index set.

## Why

Christopher chose **Cooperative for Auditable Provenance** as a canonical
institution in Hearthline's world, explicitly excluding the playful cult
framing. He placed it near the boundary between individual tasks: persistent
enough to keep the public record readable, but without sovereignty over the
people, systems, or later work that may encounter that record.

## Preserved boundaries

CAP is fictional. It is not a cult, secret order, real organization, deployed
service, hidden runtime, or claim of real-world membership. It does not
represent all people, models, or Hearthline. Christopher D. Pang remains author
and steward; AI drafting is tool assistance, not co-authorship or authority.

A CAP seal reports the trace structure and declared lineage it actually
examined. It cannot make every recorded claim true or grant permission,
jurisdiction, ownership, consent, command, or standing. CAP cannot activate
Hearthline, widen or continue a task, commission a successor task, replace a
controller, appoint an author, manufacture consent, or turn provenance into
authority. Any later reading, carrying, or action still requires the present
task's own admission and authority.

Earlier records, disputed accounts, and corrections keep their identities.
The archive rollover changes their index location, not their content, status,
chronology, adoption, effect, or reopening handles.

## Compatibility and migration

This is a lore-and-navigation successor only. It changes no agent instruction,
candidate manifest, controlling source version, runtime, campaign state,
game rule, task grant, or operational interface. Existing record links continue
to target the same stable full-record paths. The root changelog begins the
second 25-slot cohort at `HLP-000026`; older adopted rows remain readable from
the linked fixed-range index.

The branch-archive and Return Queue checkers now search the root and archived
public indexes for the historical rows they bind. That lookup change neither
rewrites those rows nor broadens either checker's substantive claims.
