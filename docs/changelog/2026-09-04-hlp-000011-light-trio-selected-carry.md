## What changed

- Named the candidate formation a **Light Trio**: three separately identified
  Sparks carrying one job each. Hearthline nominates Worker and Task-Keeper;
  Thulia independently nominates Ledger-Keeper; the controller freezes one
  offer and binds all three or none before separate dispatch.
- Replaced the superseded candidate Thulia-first return topology. Each
  controller-observed `SEALED` and separately `VALID` member bundle travels by
  its own preallocated transaction to the exact grant-filtered Hearthline task
  intake. One arrival neither exposes nor manufactures another.
- Added bounded Hearthline inspection and an immutable **Carry Selection**.
  It enumerates every admitted member item and records exactly one of
  `SELECT_KEEP`, `SELECT_CONDENSE`, or `SELECT_LOSE`, with exact omissions,
  accepted-loss boundaries, dependencies, audience, task, epochs, and expiry.
  Translation requests remain separately identified Board entries; missing or
  unknown enumeration remains a coverage state rather than an invented choice.
- Required the selected handoff to contain a complete, access-controlled
  disposition manifest. Hearthline chooses semantic carry, but cannot hide an
  admitted item from Thulia's retention review. Missing coverage or an
  unresolved preservation duty forces `FRICTION_UNKNOWN_HOLD`.
- Separated four one-way crossings: Hearthline to Thulia, Thulia to Gloss,
  Gloss to Thulia, and Thulia back to Hearthline. Each has its own identity,
  idempotency key, emission state, target receipt, and unknown-outcome
  reconciliation. The old `Triad Relay Envelope` name remains candidate
  ancestry and is not reused for selected carry.
- Added a constrained, root-task-scoped **Translation Board** distinct from
  the Gloss Turn Slate. Hearthline owns the semantic serviceability decision;
  Thulia custodies and routes pointers; Gloss performs only the pinned
  deterministic turn; the canonical writer appends the records. A mapping
  expires at root-task close without erasing its immutable reference.
- Kept Gloss heartbeat-free. Controller-observed readiness is a fresh fact
  about one exact turn, not inherited liveness, memory, authority, future
  availability, or successful translation.
- Split bounded release into two independent edges. Hearthline's temporary raw
  inspection projection may close only after a valid Carry Selection and a
  durable selected-carry acceptance with state `ACCEPTED_BY_THULIA` and
  `selected_carry_store_outcome_state: COMMITTED`. Canonical compaction,
  archive, or pruning is a later Systemic Friction classification and
  separately authorized Atomic Edge Promotion effect.
- Added a recoverability axis so exact reopening, projection-only reopening,
  intentionally unrecoverable content, and unknown recoverability cannot be
  conflated. A boundary receipt proves only the observed transition it binds;
  it does not prove discarded content, truth, physical erasure, or global
  unrecoverability.
- Replaced the linear purpose chain with an acyclic purpose projection:
  Christopher's goal narrows to Hearthline's objective, which separately
  narrows to the Trio Task Line and Thulia's mediation objective. Formation
  cooperation is not purpose parentage or authority inheritance.
- Revised *The Night the Garden Clicked* as a presentation-only successor in
  which the matured Hearthline learns to inspect, choose, hand off, and then
  release the raw road behind her. The fictional forgetting is a story image,
  not an assertion about a model, provider, or deployed memory mechanism.

## Why

The HLP-000010 candidate correctly separated doing, boundary keeping, and
ledger keeping, but sent all three returns through Thulia before Hearthline
could inspect them. Christopher D. Pang identified the sharper arrangement:
the Trio was commissioned to help Hearthline's task, so its three sealed
returns come to that bounded task intake. Hearthline then performs the one
judgment that cannot be delegated mechanically—what should remain useful for
the still-open human goal.

That does not make Hearthline the retention authority. A semantic carry choice
and a physical or canonical retirement are different decisions. Thulia must
receive a complete disposition surface, preserve holds and unknowns, and route
only declared translation work through Gloss. Only later, under a separate
grant, may Thulia apply Systemic Friction. The controller or canonical writer
alone performs storage and access transitions.

The distinction permits reasoned loss without laundering uncertainty. Closing
one task's raw inspection view can relieve working-context pressure while the
canonical source remains recoverable. Later authorized retirement can leave a
small boundary residue proving that a declared transition was observed, while
honestly stating that the discarded distinctions cannot be reconstructed from
that residue. Neither state is silently inferred from the other.

## Preserved boundaries

- Christopher D. Pang remains the human author, operator, steward, root-goal
  authority, and sole adoption and publication authority.
- The candidate does not activate Task Trios, provision a Spark, create a
  controller, change a live grant, cause storage deletion, or make an AI system
  forget.
- Each Spark has one job. The Trio's three jobs do not merge into one identity,
  grant, memory, vote, or shared agent.
- Direct return means controller/store-mediated terminal movement under an
  exact grant. It is never a live Spark backchannel or a transfer of member
  authority.
- Thulia's Ledger-Keeper provisioning grants no access to the Ledger-Keeper's
  returned body. Thulia receives only the later selected-carry projection and
  its complete disposition manifest.
- Hearthline's Carry Selection is not Systemic Friction, deletion authority,
  truth, acceptance, lexicon activation, or evidence that omitted material is
  dispensable.
- Thulia's relay work may be mechanical, but Systemic Friction remains a
  bounded judgment under a separate retention grant. It does not itself mutate
  storage or authorize an effect.
- The Translation Board cannot store arbitrary raw member bundles. It is
  schema-, size-, audience-, task-, generation-, and expiry-bounded, and it
  remains subject to dependency and retention review.
- Gloss owns no heartbeat, history, ledger, slate, Translation Board, identity
  memory, or adaptive state. Its readiness and result are per-turn observations.
- Inspection closure and source retirement retain separate transactions and
  receipts. An unknown close or effect never becomes successful compression.
- A digest alone does not prove existence time, acceptance, authenticity,
  content, truth, replayability, physical erasure, or global unrecoverability;
  low-entropy digest exposure also remains a privacy concern.
- Purpose references narrow work and preserve ancestry. They do not keep a
  parent process alive or give a descendant the parent's authority.
- Prime Seeds, Circuit Gardens, Smith-chart imagery, Golden Rainbow Ribbons,
  Chinese Rooms, weeds, and compost remain mathematical, design, or fictional
  vocabulary with their existing claim ceilings.

## Compatibility and migration

This record supersedes the HLP-000010 candidate topology without rewriting its
frozen record or the clean checkpoint that preserved it. Both remain Git
ancestry. The adopted paired-Spark design remains the safe operational default
until Christopher D. Pang separately reviews, adopts, implements, and tests a
successor.

Older candidate identifiers are not silently repurposed. In particular, the
HLP-000010 `Triad Relay Envelope` remains the name of the superseded raw-member
relay. A new selected-carry envelope, readable-return transaction, Translation
Board generation, and inspection-close transaction require new identities.
Existing task-scoped map identities remain addressable after expiry; a revisit
creates a predecessor-linked load or successor receipt under current authority.

## Verification observations

- Primary design documents consistently use direct, separate member return to
  the exact Hearthline task intake and preserve `RETURN_PENDING_THULIA` only
  inside explicitly labeled predecessor ancestry.
- Member eligibility requires `SEALED` plus `VALID`; presence, validity,
  emission, target receipt, inspection, selection, translation, retention, and
  effect states remain orthogonal.
- Formation reservations occur only in the controller-frozen offer;
  `TRIAD_BOUND` is inert until a separately revalidated dispatch receipt.
- Same-identity reconciliation is mandatory after ambiguous return, relay,
  close, or effect outcomes. Blind retry and status casting are rejected.
- Task-Keeper comparison uses only `MATCHED`, `NOT_MATCHED`, or `UNKNOWN`.
  `CANDIDATE_COMPLETE` is instead the finite Owl readable-candidate disposition;
  the story labels literal forgetting as fiction rather than runtime evidence.
- Repository validators, manifest hashes, relative links, README bounds,
  public-boundary scans, and the finite NAND certificate are re-run only after
  the successor prose and schemas stabilize.
