## What changed

The [formal companion](../../research/context-parallax/lean/README.md) adds
33 declarations in five Lean modules: capsule decoding, protected return,
finite rational optional information and diagnostic counterexamples, and
question-relative resolution. The package carries pinned dependencies,
statement and axiom reports, source hashes and kernel replay observations.
The public entrance links directly to its scope and results.

## Why

Chris requested publication of the completed local CPX Lean work in the
Hearthline repository. The companion makes the selected checks inspectable
without publishing the unpublished manuscript or the private research shelf.

## Preserved boundaries

The four claim families retain inherited attribution. These formalizations do
not establish mathematical novelty or verify the whole manuscript. The
source-to-formal comparison is a self-review. The model pilot, language
sustainability hypotheses and private experience are outside these statements.

## Compatibility and migration

No MCP tools, dependencies, installed environments or proof-source bytes are
changed by the publication step. The runner defaults to preparation inspection;
Lean execution requires its explicit run option and declared inputs. Frozen
execution records retain their original local publication status as history.
The planned public-lab/private-Cabin organization is a separate future change.

## Verification observations

The completed local execution compiled all 33 named declarations and replayed
all five modules through Lean's own kernel checker. Exact axiom dependencies
were recorded. The publication preparation rechecked the recorded proof and
report hashes and kept their Git object bytes unchanged. Repository CI reports
its structural checks separately for the published commit; it does not rerun
these Lean proofs.

## Open residuals

Kernel replay trusts the pinned cached imports and uses Lean's own checker.
It is not an independent kernel implementation, a fresh Mathlib audit, or an
independent review of the natural-language translation. The finite rational
information result and finite passive-sequence controls have the limits
stated in the [results](../../research/context-parallax/lean/RESULTS.md).

## Evidence and exclusions

The [evidence summary](../../research/context-parallax/lean/audit/RESULT.json)
and [actual axiom report](../../research/context-parallax/lean/audit/statements-and-axioms.txt)
identify the checked targets. Failed compilation and reporting attempts remain
explained in the results rather than being relabeled successful. The public
copy excludes private filesystem paths, raw conversation material and the
unpublished manuscript. Publication initiates no model experiment or training.
